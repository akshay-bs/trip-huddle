# Working on trip-huddle

trip-huddle is a Claude plugin (Cowork and Claude Code) plus an Obsidian vault layout for planning group trips. See `README.md` for what it does and `docs/ROADMAP.md` for what's next.

## Layout
- `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` and `marketplace.json`: keep the version numbers in sync.
- `docs/PLAN-*.md`: design notes for features, with a status line at the top.
- `scripts/`: shared Node scripts (`validate.mjs`, the checker; `trip-settings.mjs`, the shared reader for `trip.md`, `planning_limits` and `road_cutoffs`; `test.mjs`). `skills/add-place/scripts/validate.mjs` is only a wrapper that runs the shared one.
- `skills/<name>/SKILL.md`: plugin skills (add-place, add-source, build-itinerary, compare-itineraries, group-decisions, plan-day, review-travel-times, trip-init). Refer to bundled files as `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/<name>/...`. Skills run with the user's vault as the working directory.
- `vault-template/`: the empty starter vault.
- `example-vault/`: a filled example used to test the skills and validator.
- `vault-template/Templates`, `vault-template/Bases` and `vault-template/.obsidian/*.json` are copied into `example-vault/`. Change them in both places, together. That includes `Bases/Food.base` and `Templates/Food Spot.md` (only the `price_band` line of the Food Spot template differs, set by `trip-init`).
- Note types and their folders: `Locations/`, `Stays/` and `Food/` (restaurants and cafes) hold the places the group decides on; `Itinerary/` (Day, Route, the Trip itinerary and the Operator brief), `Sources/`, `Decisions/`, `Logistics/` and `Bookings/` hold the plan. `Logistics/legs.json` is the shared distance file of `build-itinerary` (road facts only, never decisions); the example vault has none. `Confirmations/` holds vouchers and pasted emails; the checker does not check it, and the example keeps it empty. The folders are in `trip.md` (`folders`) and the checker. The example vault's Food notes have no coordinates (none could be verified), so the checker gives them a warning; that is intended.

## Checks
After any change to templates, Bases, `types.json`, `trip-init`, its field packs, the checker, `trip.md` settings, `sun.mjs`, `travel.mjs` or `route.mjs`, run:
```bash
node scripts/test.mjs
```
It must end with "All tests passed". It checks that the example vault has 0 errors, that `trip-init --reconfigure` leaves the example vault unchanged ("Nothing changed"), that a new vault made from the Portugal answers passes the checker and gets `Route.md` and `Transport.md`, that the `plan-day` sun script (`skills/plan-day/scripts/sun.mjs`) gives known sunrise and sunset times and the same slack verdicts as the Itinerary base, that the made-up Day note in `skills/plan-day/references/` passes the checker, that the after-dark value lists and the Day note warning (an `after_dark: avoid` place or leg after sunset while `rule_bends` is 0) work, that `skills/add-place/scripts/migrate-after-dark.mjs` brings a pre-0.6 copy of the example to what the starter vault has and does nothing the second time, and that the `review-travel-times` script (`skills/review-travel-times/scripts/travel.mjs`) gives the known road-type times for both vehicle classes, the stops buffer, the `road_speeds` and `stop_buffer` overrides from `trip.md`, and the verdicts (slack, after-dark driving, agreed bends, late arrival, closed road), that the made-up checked Day note in `skills/review-travel-times/references/` passes the checker and matches the script's numbers, that the `travel_verdict` warning (better than the slack verdict) fires and doesn't, that `trip-init` writes and keeps the travel settings and rejects bad ones, that `planning_limits` in `trip.md` change the slack verdict, the travel verdicts (dark driving, late finish) and the checker's `travel_verdict` warning, reach the Itinerary base through `trip-init` (and leave the example unchanged at the defaults), and are kept or merged by `trip-init --reconfigure` and refused when bad, that Comparison notes are checked (2 or more options, a valid status, `decided_option` one of the options and only when decided) and the made-up comparison in `skills/compare-itineraries/references/` passes the checker with driving figures that match the travel script, that Food notes work (the template's `kind`, `meal` and `scheduled_day`, `Bases/Food.base` and its views and columns, the home page embed, the example's Food notes, the checker's value-list, map box and source checks, and `trip-init --reconfigure --add-food` bringing an older vault up to date and then changing nothing), that both manifests have the same version, and that a deliberately broken copy of the example has every problem reported. It works on temporary copies and leaves the repo alone.

Group decisions (v0.10.0): `Templates/Decision.md` (kept in `Decisions/`) and `Bases/Decisions.base` are in both vaults, and `trip.md` has `stage`. The tests also check that Decision notes are checked (a valid status, `decided_option` one of the options and only once decided or locked, a locked note has `locked_by`) and the conflict warnings (a locked stay or place that is not `selected`, a locked route that is not agreed, a dropped note in `affects`, locked dates while `dates_fixed` is false, a bad `stage`), that the example's Decision notes pass, that `trip-init` writes the stage, keeps it on `--reconfigure` and refuses a bad one, and that the five skills that plan or add read locked decisions.

Booking list (v0.11.0): `Templates/Booking.md` (kept in `Bookings/`), `Bases/Bookings.base`, the booking fields on Stay and Transport (Transport also has `decision`), and `needs_booking` on Location are in both vaults. The tests also check that the base lists selected stays, the selected vehicle, selected places with `needs_booking` and the Bookings notes; that the example holds no confirmations, references or amounts; the booking warnings ("booked, no confirmation yet", paid with a balance, amounts that don't add up, a booking on something not selected, a booked item whose Decision is not locked, a `locked_by` link to something not booked, a place's fields not matching `needs_booking`, a confirmation outside `Confirmations/`); that the drive limit comes from the selected Transport note; that `trip-init --reconfigure --add-bookings` and `skills/group-decisions/scripts/migrate-bookings.mjs` bring an older vault up to date and then change nothing; and that the skills read the new fields.

Build itinerary (v0.12.0): `Templates/Route.md` has a *Day by day* table and *What it makes possible / Red flags / What it costs*; `Templates/Trip Itinerary.md` (type `itinerary`) and `Templates/Operator Brief.md` (type `brief`) are new, both kept in `Itinerary/`; Day Plan has `start_time`, `backtrack_km` and *Along the way*; Location has `needs_night_nearby`; the Itinerary and Locations Bases show them; `trip.md` has `road_cutoffs` and `planning_limits.on_the_way_min`. All of it is in both vaults (every example place has `needs_night_nearby`). The tests also check that `skills/build-itinerary/scripts/route.mjs` finds the road-cutoff break and the doubling back in the made-up standard loop and neither in its reverse (`skills/build-itinerary/references/`, applied to a copy of the example with `example-setup.json`), that `candidates` finds Krang Suri on the way for a Jowai → Dawki day with no fixed reach (the daylight, `on_the_way_min` and the Transport limit change the answer; a day with less than `slack_ok_hrs` to spare is listed as a tight candidate, like the reverse loop's Krem Chympe day trip from Jowai, and a too-much day is left out), that `links` adds rough links and never overwrites a researched one (and a second run changes nothing), `link` and `town`, the checker's warnings (a bad `road_cutoffs` entry, a Day note leg in a cutoff area after its time with `rule_bends` 0, a Route Day by day table that does not match its nights, a Trip itinerary or Operator brief that misses a Day note or links a dropped place, a brief with food or a confirmation, a bad `legs.json`), the cutoff verdict in `travel.mjs` (too much from the first unagreed minute, tight when agreed as a rule bend; after-dark driving keeps its 30-minute tight band), that the Trip itinerary and Operator brief examples pass, that `trip-init` writes, keeps, replaces and refuses `road_cutoffs`, that `trip-init --reconfigure --add-itinerary` and `migrate-itinerary.mjs` bring an older vault up to date and then change nothing, and that the skills describe the new pieces.

Booking deadlines (v0.13.0): `payment_due` and `cancel_by` (both date) on the Booking, Stay and Transport templates, and in the Location FIELD GUIDE when `needs_booking` is true. `Bases/Bookings.base` has a *By date* view; `00 Home.md` has a *Deadlines coming up* embed. All of it is in both vaults (the example has empty dates). The tests also check that the templates carry the new fields, that the base has the view, that the example has no dates filled, the deadline warnings (payment overdue, free cancellation expired, cancellation before payment, date set on a non-quoted/booked note), that `trip-init --reconfigure --add-deadlines` brings an older vault up to date and then changes nothing, and that the skills mention the fields.

The shared checker is `scripts/validate.mjs` (it reads the vault's own `Templates/`, so a template change changes what is checked). Run it by hand with `cd example-vault && node ../scripts/validate.mjs`.

## Privacy rules for this repo
- The example came from a real trip and its private details were removed. Never add trip dates, group size or makeup, people's names, booking references, flight details, quotes from friends or agencies, or private messages.
- Example notes point to `[[Example - Public research]]` only.
- Photos: link to openly licensed images (Wikimedia Commons) with a credit line. Never commit image files, and never link copyrighted hotel photos in the example.
