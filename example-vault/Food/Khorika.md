# Khorika

> [!question] Group decision: open
> Added from [[Example - Public research]]. The group hasn't discussed it yet. Keep `decision: considering` until the group decides.

> [!warning] Not fully checked
> Sources disagree on where it is (a first-floor spot in Kamal C Plaza, Ulubari, on GS Road; older reviews say Silpukhuri or near the Bora Service bus stand). The map pin, veg options, price band and current hours are left empty until someone confirms them on a map or by phone.

## Why
- A well-known place in Guwahati for a sit-down Assamese meal, so a good first or last meal of the trip.
- The Assamese thali is the usual order, and reviewers describe the veg thali as simple and flavourful.

## Must-try dishes
- The Assamese thali (veg and non-veg versions exist)

## Practical
- **Seating for the whole group?** Not known. Ask when booking.
- **Reservation needed?** Not known. A group of this size should call ahead.
- **Payment methods (cash / card / other):** Not known.
- One review criticises hygiene, so read recent reviews before choosing it.
