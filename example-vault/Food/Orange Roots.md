# Orange Roots

> [!question] Group decision: open
> Added from [[Example - Public research]]. The group hasn't discussed it yet. Keep `decision: considering` until the group decides.

> [!warning] Not fully checked
> Sources give slightly different addresses (Khliehshnong, on the Sohra-Shella road, just before Sohra town). The map pin, price band and current hours are left empty. It is a vegetarian restaurant, not a place for Khasi food.

## Why
- Described as the most famous vegetarian restaurant in Sohra, so it solves the veg question for a meal in the area.
- Reviewers praise the food and the cleanliness.

## Must-try dishes
- Dosas and uttapams, and the lunch thali
- Breakfast items such as puri with sabji, and chole bhature

## Practical
- **Seating for the whole group?** Not known.
- **Reservation needed?** Not known. Ask ahead for a group.
- **Payment methods (cash / card / other):** Not known.
- For Khasi dishes such as jadoh or smoked pork, look at local eateries instead.
