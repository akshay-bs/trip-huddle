# Amchong Tea Estate

> [!warning] Not a free walk, and plucking is over in December
> Some itineraries say "Tea walk free". It isn't: Amchong Leaf sells booked packages, roughly ₹900–2,400 per person. In mid-December, plucking has stopped and the bushes are pruned for winter, so the gardens look less lush and the factory may be idle. Book ahead and ask what a December visit includes.

## Overview
A working tea estate of about 1,780 acres run by the Khemka family since 1958, in the low hills near Sonapur, east of Guwahati. Its visitor centre, **Amchong Leaf**, calls itself India's first tea experience centre. It offers guided garden walks, a factory tour, tea tasting, a 360° audio-visual show and the **Steep House** restaurant.

## Why go
- It's the easiest way to see an Assam tea garden on this trip, because the classic estates (Jorhat, Dibrugarh) are far east.
- **Adults:** tasting (white, oolong, green, masala and CTC), the factory process and good tea to buy.
- **Teens:** photos among the bushes, the hilltop gazebo and, by some reports, ATV and jeep rides on the estate (confirm).
- It breaks up the airport → Pobitora drive on arrival day with a proper lunch.

## Season reality
The weather is dry and comfortable, which suits the walk. The catch is the season: plucking usually ends by late November or early December and the bushes are cut back over winter. The garden will be green but not in flush, and there may be no leaf being processed in the factory. The experience centre, tastings and restaurant run year-round.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** a guided estate walk of about 1 hr inside a 2–4 hr package. No published distance.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** estate paths between tea rows on gentle slopes.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none reported.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. The restaurant and experience centre are next to the gardens.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** NH-27 to Sonapur, then the Sonapur–Digaru road (the "Jubai No. 2, Digaru Gaon" address). Coordinates are from the Peek activity listing, not from OpenStreetMap, so check the pin with the estate before the day.

## Activities
- Guided tea-garden walk and hilltop gazebo
- Factory tour (from plucked leaf to packed tea) and tasting session
- 360° audio-visual show on the history of tea
- Lunch or hi-tea at Steep House
- Shop at the Amchong Leaf store

## Tips
- Phone numbers are on the site's contact page.
- **Pure-veg group:** Steep House serves meat. Ask for a vegetarian set menu when booking. The PDF's "Club Nirvana Pure Veg (Sonapur)" was not verified.
- That leaves about 1 hr of driving to Pobitora, finishing around dusk.

## Nearby / combine with
- Guwahati sights ([[Kamakhya Temple]], [[Umananda Temple]]) are at the other end of the trip.
