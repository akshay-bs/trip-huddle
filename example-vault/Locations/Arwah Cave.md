# Arwah Cave

## Overview
A limestone cave above Lumshynna village, about 3.5 km from Sohra (Cherrapunji) town. The cave system is believed to run for about 3 km, but visitors see a lit section of roughly 300 m, with fossils of ancient marine life set into the rock walls.

## Why go
- The walk in is part of the fun. A paved path runs through forest along the cliff side, with views over the Sohra gorges and the Nohkalikai side of the plateau.
- Teens: a proper cave feel with dark chambers, dripping rock and fossil hunting, but with a fixed path and lighting.
- Adults: less cramped and less crowded than [[Mawsmai Cave]], and the forest walk itself is scenic.

## Season reality
December is a good time to go. The cave and path are drier and less slippery than in the monsoon, and there is no fog issue inside. It is cold and damp in the chambers, so wear a jacket. Crowds rise around Christmas week but it is usually calmer than Mawsmai.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** Paved walkway from the parking area to the cave mouth. Sources disagree on the length, from about 400 m up to about 1 km. Most say 10–15 min each way. Plan about 1.5 km and 60–75 min round trip including the cave loop (about 300 m inside, 30–45 min).
- **Surface / steps / railings:** Stone-paved path with some steps and a walkway/bridge built along the hillside. Railings on many stretches. Inside, the tourist section has a path, railings and lights in the entry chambers.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** Wet rock and puddles inside, even in the dry season. A few low-ceiling or narrow turns where you need to stoop or turn sideways, but no crawling on the standard route. Deeper parts get darker, so carry a torch or use phone lights.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. Anyone who does not want the walk can wait at the parking area or stalls.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** Paved road to the parking lot; the tempo traveller can reach it.

## Activities
- Forest walk to the cave with gorge views
- Explore the lit cave section and spot fossils (shells and marine impressions) in the walls
- Photos at the cave mouth and along the cliff walkway

## Tips
- Wear shoes with good grip, not sandals. The cave floor is wet and uneven.
- Carry a small torch; phone torches work too.
- Go early. Afternoon light fades fast in December (sunset about 4:30 pm).
- Anyone claustrophobic can see the big entrance chamber and turn back; the tight bits are further in.
- Optional local guides at the gate point out fossils you would otherwise miss.
- Best time, as first written: Morning (9–11 am), before tour groups arrive

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Nohkalikai Falls]] and [[Wei Sawdong Falls]] are on the same (west) side of Sohra.
- [[Mawsmai Cave]] and [[Seven Sisters Falls]] if you want a second cave; most groups pick one of the two caves.
