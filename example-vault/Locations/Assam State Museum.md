# Assam State Museum

## Overview
Assam's main state museum, founded in 1940 by the Kamarupa Anusandhan Samiti and run by the state government since 1953. It is at the south end of Dighalipukhuri tank in central Guwahati. Collections cover archaeology (stone and bronze sculptures from ancient Kamarupa), inscriptions, manuscripts, coins, arms, textiles and ethnographic displays, including life-size models of tribal village life.

## Why go
- A compact, cheap way to see Assam's history, from ancient Kamarupa to the Ahom kingdom.
- **Teens:** the ethnographic section with village reconstructions and costumes is the most engaging part.
- **Adults:** sculptures, manuscripts and Ahom-era artefacts.
- About 1 km from Umananda Ghat, so it fits easily into the city-tour morning.

## Season reality
No seasonal issues. Just check the closure days: Mondays, 2nd and 4th Saturdays and government holidays.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** indoor galleries, about 1 hr.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** tiled floors; stairs between floors.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at Dighalipukhuri park across the road.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** busy central road; drop and pick up at the gate.

## Activities
- Archaeology and sculpture galleries
- Ethnographic gallery with village reconstructions
- Stroll around Dighalipukhuri tank afterwards

## Tips
- Last ticket at 4 PM.
- Photography may be restricted or charged in some galleries.
- One museum may be enough: if time is short, choose between this and [[Srimanta Sankardev Kalakshetra]]. This one is more history; Kalakshetra is more culture and open space.
