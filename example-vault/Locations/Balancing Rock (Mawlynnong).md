# Balancing Rock (Mawlynnong)

## Overview
A natural curiosity: a large boulder resting at an odd angle on a much smaller stone, now enclosed by a wire fence. Locally it is called **Maw Ryngkew Sharatia**. OpenStreetMap places it on the outskirts of Mawlynnong in Nohwet village land (the Zingmaham locality), about 1 km from Mawlynnong and 1 km short of the Riwai root bridge. Some blogs wrongly say it is about 5 km away. Nohwet/Mawlynnong is correct.

## Why go
- A 10-minute "how is that still standing?" stop on the road between [[Mawlynnong Village]] and [[Riwai Living Root Bridge]]. It is on the way, so it adds almost no time.
- Adults: the local lore. One story says it was a place of ancient sacrifices to a deity. Guides describe it as an old Khasi shrine abandoned after Christianity came to the area. Treat both as folklore.
- Teens: a quick photo. On its own it isn't worth a detour.

## Season reality
Weather makes no difference here. December is dry and pleasant. It is a small site, so a few tour vehicles arriving together make it feel busy for 10 minutes. Verdict: good as a quick add-on. It is a minor sight, so keep it only if it's on the way.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** about 100 m each way, around 10 min round trip.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** a short path with roughly 50 steps from the ticket point (per travel guides). The rock itself is fenced.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none significant.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, easily. It can be seen quickly or skipped from the vehicle.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** on the village road between Mawlynnong and Riwai/Nohwet. The tempo traveller is fine.

## Activities
- Look and take photos (5–10 min)
- Hear the local story from the ticket keeper or a guide

## Tips
- Visit it in the same loop as the root bridge. Ask the driver to stop on the way to or from Riwai.
- Don't climb on or lean against the rock or the fence.
- Keep small change for the ₹10–20 ticket.
- Best time, as first written: Any daylight time. Combine with the early-morning root bridge visit
