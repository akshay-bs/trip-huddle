# Blue Lagoon (Nongriat)

> [!question] Which Blue Lagoon?
> Several places in Meghalaya are called "Blue Lagoon". This note assumes the natural pool on the Nongriat trail, since it was asked for together with [[Rainbow Falls]] and the [[Double Decker Living Root Bridge]]. Other places sold under the same name:
> - [[Labon Falls]] in East Jaintia Hills (now its own note) (near Saipung, about 140 km from Shillong; a 45–60 min forest trek and possibly a 4x4).
> - An unnamed "Blue Lagoon" in East Khasi Hills on the Dawki side, described as a short walk from the road.
> - Some sites also call the pool at the foot of [[Rainbow Falls]] itself the Blue Lagoon.
>
> If a different one was meant, change this note.

## Overview
A natural pool of clear, bright blue water in the forest beyond Nongriat village, below Sohra. It is on the trail from the [[Double Decker Living Root Bridge]] towards [[Rainbow Falls]], about 30 min past the Double Decker. Exact coordinates were not found.

## Why go
- A swimming hole with very clear blue water, quieter than the pools at the Double Decker.
- A good turnaround point for trekkers who want more than the Double Decker but not the full Rainbow Falls trek.
- **Teens:** a (very cold) swim.

## Season reality
Dry season, so the water is at its clearest and bluest and the trail is dry. The water is icy, so a swim will be quick. Reaching it adds about an hour to the Double Decker round trip, which is still possible in a December day with an early start from Tyrna.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** Tyrna → Double Decker bridge, 1.5–2 hrs down. Then about 30 min more on the trail towards Rainbow Falls. Then back up to Tyrna (2–3 hrs). Plan about 6–7 hrs of walking round trip.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** the Nongriat stone steps, then forest paths and rocks down to the pool.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** wet rocks around the pool; deep water.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at the Double Decker bridge (or at Tyrna).
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** paved road to Tyrna parking; tempo traveller OK.

## Activities
- Swim in the pool (cold; only where the guide says it is safe)
- Rest stop on the way to [[Rainbow Falls]]

## Tips
- Same rules as the Double Decker trek: not a fit for the group's "no long treks" rule; optional for the fittest members only.
- Bring a towel and a change of clothes if swimming.
- Ask a Tyrna guide or Nongriat locals for the "Blue Lagoon" turn-off.
- Best time, as first written: Late morning, when the sun is on the water

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Double Decker Living Root Bridge]] (30 min back) and [[Rainbow Falls]] (further along the same trail) are all one trek.
