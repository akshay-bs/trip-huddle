# Brahmaputra Sunset Cruise

## Overview
This is a 1-hour sunset cruise on the Brahmaputra from Guwahati's riverfront (MG Road, Panbazar/Fancy Bazar). The best-known operator is **Alfresco Grand**, a multi-deck river boat with a bar, restaurant, live singer and DJ. In winter it boards from the new **Gateway of Guwahati** floating terminal (opened Nov 2025) and in summer from Uzanbazar. The cruise passes Umananda (Peacock) island temple and the Saraighat bridge area.

## Why go
- It is a relaxed, zero-effort evening after a long drive down from Shillong, with good views of the river at its widest.
- Teens: music, a DJ, decks to roam and snacks on board. Adults: sunset views, a drink, and Umananda island and the city skyline.
- It works well as a closing-evening group activity.

## Season reality
- December is dry season, so the river is calm and lower. It is a comfortable month for cruising.
- Sunset in Guwahati in late December is around 4:25 PM, which is why winter sailings leave around 4:00 PM. A late arrival from Shillong could miss it, so plan to reach Guwahati by 3:00 PM.
- Winter haze and smoke can make the sunset hazy and orange rather than vivid.
- It gets cool on the open upper deck after sunset (about 15 °C), so carry a jacket.
- 24 Dec (Christmas Eve) is a busy evening. Book online when bookings open (Alfresco Grand opens bookings 7 days ahead, and online booking closes 4 hrs before departure).

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** a few minutes from the drop-off through the terminal and down the jetty or gangway.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** paved terminal, floating pontoon and gangway with railings, plus internal stairs between decks.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** the gangway and pontoon can be damp. Life jackets are provided on board.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at the riverfront or the eateries nearby.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** MG Road riverfront. The tempo traveller can drop off and pick up, but parking along MG Road is limited.

## Activities
- **Alfresco Grand sunset cruise:**
  - about 4:00 PM in winter, 60 min
  - middle deck ₹700 adult / ₹600 child, upper deck ₹850 / ₹700 (fixed year-round, no weekend surge)
  - includes a live singer and DJ; food and drinks are extra
  - Phone +91 98549 58549
- **Alternatives:**
  - Star Cruise Brahmaputra boards from Kuruwa Ferry Ghat opposite JB's Sweet Shop, Fancy Bazar. It costs about ₹749 per adult for 60 min, with live music, a Bihu dance and a snack included.
  - Vista Cruise charges about ₹700 per adult and ₹600 per child (ages 4–11).
  - Assam Tourism also lists river cruises.
- Dinner cruises (later evening, about ₹1,200+) are an option if the group misses the sunset slot.

## Tips
- Check the exact December departure time when booking. Alfresco's site lists about 4:00 PM in winter, but the times shift with sunset. Report 30 min early.
- The upper deck is worth the extra ₹150 for the sunset view.
- Teens aged 14–16 will almost certainly pay the adult fare. Confirm each operator's child age limit.
- Carry a jacket or shawl for the open deck.
- Best time, as first written: The sunset sailing, about 4:00–5:00 PM in December (report 30 min early)
