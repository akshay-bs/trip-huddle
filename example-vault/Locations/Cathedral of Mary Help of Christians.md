# Cathedral of Mary Help of Christians

## Overview
The Catholic cathedral of Shillong, in Laitumkhrah. The present building dates from the late 1940s, after the earlier wooden church burned down in 1936. It is known for its pale blue-and-white facade, tall arches, stained-glass windows and terracotta Stations of the Cross. It's about 1 km from Valmere Chery Blossom Suites.

## Why go
- A short, easy stop and one of Shillong's best-known buildings.
- In the week before Christmas it gives a feel for Shillong's Christmas: lights, carols, cribs and decorated streets around Laitumkhrah.
- **Teens:** the Christmas atmosphere and cafés nearby. **Adults:** architecture and the quiet interior.

## Season reality
The run-up to Christmas is the best time of year to see it. Be respectful during Mass.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** a few minutes from the road, up a wide staircase.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** paved steps with railings.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes; cafés are nearby.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** city main road; drop-off at the gate. Parking for a tempo traveller is limited, so drop and pick up.

## Activities
- See the interior, stained glass and Stations of the Cross
- Evening walk around Laitumkhrah for Christmas lights and cafés

## Tips
- Dress modestly and keep quiet inside; photos only where allowed.
- Sunday Mass times reported as about 6:30, 8:30, 10:30 AM and 4:30 PM. Avoid those slots for sightseeing.
- It's close to the hotel anyway.
- Best time, as first written: Afternoon, or evening for Christmas lights
