# Dainthlen Falls

> [!info] One river, many falls
> [[Mawsawa Falls]], [[Janailar Falls]], [[Lyngksiar Falls]], [[Dainthlen Falls]] and [[Wei Sawdong Falls]] are all on the same stream system, west of the Shillong–Sohra road near Mawkma / Laitlyndop. Pick 2–3 easy ones rather than all.

## Overview
A wide, stepped waterfall about 10 km from Sohra. By Khasi legend, this is where people killed the **Thlen**, a giant man-eating serpent; carvings of the story are on the rocks nearby. The water flows over flat rock shelves at the top, so you can walk right up to the falls. **Entry is free.**

## Why go
- An easy, flat, roadside waterfall with a good story.
- **Teens:** the Thlen legend and the rock carvings. **Adults:** an easy stop with no big climb.

## Season reality
Flow is much thinner. The wide rock shelves are dry and easy to walk on, but the falls are less dramatic.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** a few minutes from the parking over flat rock.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** open rock shelves; no railings.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** **the edge above the drop**. Keep the group back from the lip and off wet rock.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, but it's easy for everyone.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** paved road from Sohra; the same road as the [[Wei Sawdong Falls]] turn-off.
