# David Scott Trail

## Overview
A historic trail through the Khasi Hills, built in the 1820s–30s by David Scott, the British agent for the north-east frontier, as a mule and cart route between Sylhet (now Bangladesh) and Assam. The popular trekking section runs about 16 km from **Mawphlang** to **Lad Mawphlang**, past Khasi villages, sacred groves, meadows, streams, old stone bridges and natural pools. The coordinates are OpenStreetMap's trail pin near the Mawphlang end. Walking distance and time above are one-way (the trail is point-to-point, not a round trip).

## Why go
- One of Meghalaya's best-known walks, with varied, beautiful scenery and almost no crowds.
- **Teens:** a real adventure day: streams, stone bridges, open hills.
- **Adults:** only for the fit ones who actually want a 5–6 hr hike.

## Season reality
Weather-wise December is one of the best months. But the full trail takes the whole day, leaving no time for [[Laitlum Canyons]]. If anyone does it, it should replace everything else that day.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** 16 km one way, 5–6 hrs; about 1 hr descent, 2–3 hrs flat, about 1 hr climb at the end.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** old stone-paved track, dirt paths, stream crossings on stones or bridges; no railings.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** stream crossings and mossy stones; some narrow paths on slopes.
- Non-hikers could do [[Mawphlang Sacred Forest]] at the start, then drive round to meet the hikers at Lad Mawphlang. That needs the vehicle for the whole day.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** paved roads at both ends; the pick-up at Lad Mawphlang must be arranged in advance.

## Activities
- Full trek Mawphlang → Lad Mawphlang with a local guide
- Short taster: walk the first 1–2 km from Mawphlang and back (about 1–1.5 hrs, downhill out and uphill back)

## Tips
- **Not a good fit for this group** as a whole (low-trek preference). Consider only the short taster from Mawphlang, combined with the sacred forest.
- Always walk Mawphlang → Lad Mawphlang, never the reverse.
- Carry lunch, 2 litres of water each, a light jacket, and a torch just in case.

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Mawphlang Sacred Forest]]: at the start point
