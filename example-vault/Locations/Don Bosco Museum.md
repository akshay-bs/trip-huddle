# Don Bosco Museum

## Overview
The Don Bosco Museum of Indigenous Cultures, in Mawlai, Shillong. It's a seven-storey hexagonal building with **21 themed galleries** on the peoples and cultures of the North-East, plus a rooftop **skywalk** with a 360° view of Shillong. One of the best museums in the region.

## Why go
- **Teens:** the skywalk and the costume and weapons galleries. **Adults:** the depth of the collections.

## Season reality
> [!warning] Closed dates
> **Closed every Sunday and on 22–26 Dec** (and 31 Dec). Winter hours (1 Dec – 31 Jan) are 09:30–16:30.

It's indoors, so fog and cold don't matter. A good backup for a rainy or foggy day.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** indoors, over 7 floors. There's a lift, but plan on some stairs.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** museum floors; railings on the skywalk.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at a café or in the vehicle.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** in town (Sacred Heart Road, Mawlai); any vehicle.

## Activities
- The 21 galleries (about 1.5–2 hr for a good look)
- Skywalk on the roof

## Tips
- Entry is ₹50 per person (Indian adults and students); the camera fee is ₹100.
- Go in the morning. In December the museum closes at 16:30.

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Cathedral of Mary Help of Christians]] and [[Ward's Lake]] are 10–15 min away; [[Police Bazaar]] about 15 min.
- Loft Executive Inn is nearby in Laitumkhrah.
