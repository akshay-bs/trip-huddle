# Garden of Caves

## Overview
"Ka Bri Ki Synrang" at Laitmawsiang, on the Shillong–Sohra road about 10 km before Sohra. A small eco-park with a trail through limestone caves, rock overhangs, bamboo bridges and about **14 small waterfalls** dropping into clear pools. The best-known spot is a cave with a waterfall falling across its mouth.

## Why go
- Lots of variety in a short, easy loop: caves, bridges and pools.
- **Teens:** the caves and the bamboo bridges. **Adults:** the photos, and it's far easier than the deeper Sohra waterfall treks.

## Season reality
The flow is much lower than after the monsoon, so the waterfalls are thin streams. The caves and pools are still worth it, and the trail is drier (safer). Visitors report seeing the caves and several falls in December.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** a loop of about 1–1.5 km on stone paths and steps (about 1.5 hr with stops).
- **Surface / steps / railings:** stone steps, bamboo bridges; some railings.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** wet rocks near the falls and inside the caves.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at the entrance.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** a short side road off the Shillong–Sohra road at Laitmawsiang. Check the parking for a big vehicle.

## Activities
- Cave trail and waterfalls
- Photos at the cave-mouth waterfall

## Tips
- Entry reports vary (₹20 plus ₹20 for a phone camera, up to ₹100). Carry cash.
- One source says it's open Mon–Sat only (08:30–17:30), but a newer one ([Capture A Trip](https://www.captureatrip.com/blog/garden-of-caves)) lists Sunday hours of about 09:00–17:00. Confirm before relying on a Sunday visit.
- Wear shoes with grip.
- Best time, as first written: Late morning

## Nearby / combine with
- On the Shillong → Sohra drive, near [[Mawkdok Dympep Valley Viewpoint]]
- [[Lyngksiar Falls]], [[Janailar Falls]], [[Mawsawa Falls]], [[Dainthlen Falls]] and [[Wei Sawdong Falls]] are in the same area, west of the road
