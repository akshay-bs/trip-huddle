# Indo-Bangladesh Friendship Gate

## Overview
The border gate at the Dawki (India) – Tamabil (Bangladesh) Integrated Check Post, about 90 km south of Shillong, just past Dawki bridge. It was built in 2018 for a Wagah-style ceremony by the two border forces. From the Indian side you look across into Bangladesh; nearby, the Umngot river crosses the border at Jaflong "zero point".

## Why go
- A short stop to see the international border.
- **Teens:** standing at the border and seeing Bangladesh. **Adults:** the history, and seeing the cross-border trade.

## Season reality
Dry and clear. The Dawki road and bridge jam badly in Christmas week (see [[Umngot River (Dawki)]]), so this is a 15–20 min add-on to the river visit, not a separate trip.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** short, from where the vehicle stops to the viewing area.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** road / check-post area.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** on the Dawki–Tamabil road past the bridge; trucks queue here, so parking may be tight.

## Activities

## Tips
- Follow the BSF's instructions on photography and where you can stand. Carry ID.
- Open about 6 am – 6 pm. Ask the driver whether a ceremony is held that day.
- Best time, as first written: With the Dawki river visit
