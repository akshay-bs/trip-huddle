# Janailar Falls

> [!info] One river, many falls
> [[Mawsawa Falls]], [[Janailar Falls]], [[Lyngksiar Falls]], [[Dainthlen Falls]] and [[Wei Sawdong Falls]] are all on the same stream system, west of the Shillong–Sohra road near Mawkma / Laitlyndop. Pick 2–3 easy ones rather than all.

## Overview
A small, uncommercialised waterfall near Mawkma, west of Sohra, with a natural pool just above the drop. Usually visited on a waterfall-hopping day with its neighbours.

## Why go
- Quiet and uncrowded; a natural pool above the falls.
- **Teens:** the pool. **Adults:** a calm spot away from the crowds.

## Season reality
Thin flow, but clear water. The trail is dry. Details are scarce, so treat it as optional.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** some walking from the road over uneven natural ground; moderate effort. Exact time unknown.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** unpaved trail; no railings expected.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** rocks around the pool and the lip of the falls. Keep away from the edge.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** local road near Mawkma; confirm with the driver.
