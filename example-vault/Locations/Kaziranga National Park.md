# Kaziranga National Park

## Overview
A UNESCO World Heritage Site and tiger reserve on the south bank of the Brahmaputra in central Assam, about 220–250 km (5–6 hrs) east of Guwahati. It holds about two-thirds of the world's one-horned rhinos, among tall elephant grass, marshes and beels. The park is split into safari ranges. **Central (Kohora)** is the most popular and closest to Red River Retreat; Western (Bagori), Eastern (Agoratoli) and Burapahar are the others. The coordinates above mark the Kohora road to the Central Range office, not the park centre.

## Why go
- Almost guaranteed rhino sightings, often close to the jeep track. Also wild water buffalo, swamp deer, elephants, and many birds. Tigers are present but rarely seen.
- **Teens:** the elephant-back safari at dawn through grass taller than the elephant, and open-jeep drives. This is the "big wildlife" part of the trip.
- **Adults:** no walking at all. Easy, seated sightseeing.
- A much bigger, better-known park than [[Pobitora Wildlife Sanctuary]], but it costs two extra travel days.

## Season reality
December is one of the best months. The park is open, grass is shorter after burning, and animals gather near water. Mornings are **misty and cold**, especially on the dawn elephant safari. Fog can hide animals until about 8–9 am.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** none. Jeeps drive a set circuit (about 28 km, 2 hrs in the Central Range) with stops at watchtowers.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** watchtowers have a few flights of steps (optional).
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. Anyone can skip a slot and stay at the hotel.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** paved road to the range gate; hired safari jeeps (Gypsy/open jeep) inside.

## Activities
- Elephant safari at dawn (Central Range, about 1 hr)
- Jeep safaris: morning and afternoon slots, ideally in two different ranges
- Watchtower stops for birdwatching
- Optional: [[Kaziranga Orchid and Biodiversity Park]] (evening cultural programme), tea-estate walks near Kohora

## Tips
- That's a lot for everyone, so pick 2–3. A good combination is dawn elephant + one morning jeep + one afternoon jeep in a different range.
- Plus 5% GST.
- Elephant-safari slots are allotted by the Forest Department and can't be guaranteed, so have a backup jeep slot.
- Wear muted colours; carry binoculars, a warm jacket, and a dust scarf for the jeep.
- Best time, as first written: Early morning (elephant safari 5:30 AM, first jeep slot about 7:00–7:30 AM) or the 1:30 PM afternoon jeep slot

## Nearby / combine with
- Red River Retreat: the proposed hotel, about 5 min from the Central Range gate
