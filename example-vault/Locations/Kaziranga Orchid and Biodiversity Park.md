# Kaziranga Orchid and Biodiversity Park

## Overview
Kaziranga National Orchid and Biodiversity Park, at Durgapur near Kohora, a few km from the Central Range of [[Kaziranga National Park]]. It covers about 6 acres, with 500+ orchid species plus sections for bamboo, cane, medicinal plants and rice varieties. It also has a handicrafts and culture museum, a traditional Assamese house, local food stalls and **evening Bihu / cultural dance performances**.

## Why go
- A relaxed evening activity at Kaziranga after the drive from Guwahati.
- **Teens:** the cultural dance show and the Assamese food stall. **Adults:** the orchids, the museum and the handicrafts.

## December reality
Few orchids flower in December (the main season is March–May), so the garden is greener than it is colourful. The cultural programme and the museum run all year. Evenings are cool (about 10 °C).

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** flat paved paths around the park, about 1 km in total.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** paved, level.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, but it's easy enough for everyone.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** on the main road at Kohora; any vehicle.

## Activities
- Walk the orchid and bamboo sections
- Culture museum and handicrafts
- Evening cultural programme (Bihu and tribal dances)
- Assamese snacks at the food stall

## Tips
- Entry about ₹100 per person. The park closes about 5 pm; ask what time the cultural programme starts that day.
