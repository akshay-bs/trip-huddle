# Krang Suri Falls

## Overview
A curtain-like waterfall about 40 ft high, dropping into a deep turquoise pool, near Amlarem in West Jaintia Hills. It is about 30 km (≈45 min) from Jowai, about 30 km from Dawki and roughly 90 km from Shillong. It is one of the most photographed falls in the Jaintia Hills and has proper steps, changing rooms and a café.

## Why go
- The colour: the pool is a vivid blue-green that changes with the light, and the wide curtain of water looks great in photos.
- **Teens:** a swim or boat ride in the pool with a life jacket, and the path that goes behind and around the falls (where open).
- **Adults:** a well-built path, a viewpoint partway down, a café at the top. It is one of the easier "real" waterfalls in Meghalaya.

## Season reality
The best season for the colour. The flow is lower and calmer than in the monsoon (a gentle curtain rather than a thundering torrent), and the pool is clearest and most vivid, especially around midday sun. Swimming and boating are regulated with compulsory life jackets and are restricted in the monsoon, not in winter. The water is cold (air temperatures are about 5–15 °C), so a swim will be short. It is popular with locals and tourists, so expect moderate crowds; weekends and the 24 Dec – 1 Jan week are busier. No official closed day was found in any source.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** from the parking area, a stone path with about **200–250 steps** down to the pool: 10–15 min down, up to 25 min back up. Plan about 40 min of walking round trip, plus time at the pool. One source calls it a "30-minute trek" and "steep", so plan for a real climb back up.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** stone-carved or paved steps; a small stone bridge over the stream; bamboo benches and a viewpoint on the way.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** spray-wet steps and rocks near the pool, and the path behind the falls. Rocks under the water are slippery.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. They can wait at the café by the parking area, or stop at the viewpoint partway down (which still gives a view of the falls).
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** paved road via Jowai → Amlarem to the parking area; a tempo traveller should manage (confirm with the driver).

## Activities
- Walk down to the pool; photos from the viewpoint and the base
- Swimming in the pool with a compulsory life jacket (about ₹100). The water is cold in December.
- Boat ride on the pool (about ₹100 per person, life jacket required)
- Tea or coffee at the café by the parking area

## Tips
- Entry fee reports vary from ₹20 to ₹50 per person (₹40 is the most specific recent figure); carry cash.
- Swimmers: carry a towel, a change of clothes and a warm layer; use the changing rooms. Life jackets on at all times; swim only in the permitted zone.
- Shoes with grip for the steps and rocks.
- Go before 2 pm; the valley loses sun early in December.

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Phe Phe Falls]] is about 8 km away in a straight line (further by road) in the same Amlarem area.
- [[Umngot River (Dawki)]] and [[Shnongpdeng]] are about 30 km south.
