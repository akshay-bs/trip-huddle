# Krem Chympe Falls

## Overview
A waterfall at the mouth of Krem Chympe, a limestone river cave near Khaddum and Brichyrnot villages in East Jaintia Hills. It is about 125–130 km (4–5 hrs) from Shillong, 55–70 km (2–2.5 hrs) from Jowai and about 40 km (1.5 hrs) from Khliehriat. "Krem" means cave. The milky-white falls drop off a limestone cliff into an emerald pool a few metres outside the cave. The cave runs for about 20 km and has a crystal-clear underground river and about 50 natural rock dams, some 12 m high. Some sites call the place Khaddum Falls.

## Why go
- **The falls:** a curtain of water over a limestone cliff into an emerald pool. You can see it from a bamboo bridge without entering the cave.
- **The cave:** a bamboo raft ride with a guide into the dark river passage, with calcite formations and underground waterfalls.
- **Teens:** a real adventure (4x4 ride, suspension bridge, raft, cave).
- **Adults:** a beautiful, remote spot with few visitors, but only for those happy with a long, rough day.

## Season reality
December is in the right season. The cave is closed in the monsoon because of flash floods, and in the dry months the river is clear turquoise and the raft trip runs. A dry track also means the 4x4 pickup can usually drive most of the way, which cuts the walk sharply. If it has rained recently, the track becomes impassable and the walk grows to 7–8 km each way. The falls are thinner than after the monsoon. The water is very cold, so anyone going into the cave will get soaked in cold water. Sunset is around 4:30 pm, and the round trip (drive plus walk plus cave) takes most of a day.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** with the 4x4 pickup, sources give 20 min to 1.5 hrs each way, depending on where the vehicle stops. Without it, the trek is 7–8 km and 2.5–3 hrs each way. I've put 90 min round trip in the properties, which assumes the pickup runs.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** a forest trail with a hanging suspension bridge 10–15 min in, boulder crossings, some rock scrambling and a rock slope down to the water's edge. One account calls it "not technical" but "not a flat walk either".
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** wet rocks along the trail and at the pool. Inside the cave, the path is narrow and wet and there is deep water (life jackets are provided).
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Not easily. There are no facilities at the trailhead. Non-swimmers who make the walk can watch from the bamboo bridge by the falls instead of going into the cave.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** from the Lumshnong toll plaza on NH-6, turn left onto a rough, steep dirt road to Brichyrnot (about 7 km). This needs a 4x4 or a high-clearance SUV, not the tempo traveller. Hire local 4x4 pickups at Brichyrnot.

## Activities
- View the falls from the bamboo bridge
- Bamboo raft ride into the cave with a guide (about 200 m by raft, then on foot and some crawling; about 2–2.5 hrs)
- Cross the suspension bridge on the trail
- Swim at the pool where the guide allows (very cold)

## Tips
- Book a guide in advance; one is mandatory and the site is not on Google Maps.
- Wear clothes and shoes you don't mind soaking, with good grip. Bring a full change of dry clothes, a towel and a warm layer.
- Carry a waterproof phone pouch, a head torch (guides usually have them), water and snacks; nothing is sold on the trail.
- Check the weather: if it has rained in the previous days, the 4x4 track may be shut and the walk doubles.
- Anyone with knee problems or a fear of deep water should stop at the falls viewpoint.
- **Driving after dark:** local drivers told the group (3 Oct 2026) that they don't drive in this area after 4:30 pm. That rules out a day trip from Shillong or Mawlynnong, but not a trip from a Jowai or Khliehriat base that is back by 15:00–16:00.
- Best time, as first written: Start very early (leave Khliehriat about 6:30 am) and be out of the forest well before 4 pm

## Nearby / combine with
- It is far east of every other place on the list. It would take a full day from Jowai, or an overnight in Khliehriat.
- [[Moopun Falls]] is on the same NH-6 corridor (Kongong junction, between Jowai and Khliehriat), so the two could share a day if the group stays near Khliehriat.
- [[Krang Suri Falls]] and [[Phe Phe Falls]] are south of Jowai, in a different direction.
- [[Labon Falls]] is about 13 km north (straight line), near Saipung; similar remote access with guides and 4x4s.
