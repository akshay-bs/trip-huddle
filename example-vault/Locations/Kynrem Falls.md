# Kynrem Falls

## Overview
A three-tier waterfall, about **305 m** in total (one of the tallest in India), inside Thangkharang Park, about 12 km south of Sohra near Laitkynsew. You see it from the park's viewpoints across the gorge; there's no walk to the falls.

## Why go
- Big views from the park: Kynrem across the gorge and the Bangladesh plains below.
- A quick stop if the route goes via Laitkynsew.

## Season reality
Monsoon-fed. **In December it is usually very thin or dry**, like [[Seven Sisters Falls]]. The views of the plains from Thangkharang Park are still good on a clear day.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** a few minutes from the parking to the viewpoints.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** paved park paths.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** on the Sohra–Laitkynsew road; any vehicle.

## Activities

## Tips
- Park entry is about ₹10–30.
- In December, treat it as a 20-minute stop, not a destination.
- Best time, as first written: Morning (clearer before the haze and fog)
