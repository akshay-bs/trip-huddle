# Labon Falls

> [!warning] In December this is a blue pool, not a waterfall
> The 30 m falls are fed by a monsoon stream. A December visitor reported "no waterfall", just the blue water. The pool is the attraction in winter, and it means a 4–5 hr drive each way from Shillong plus a 2 hr forest trek.

## Overview
A 30 m seasonal waterfall and a vivid blue pool at the mouth of **Krem Labon**, a limestone resurgence cave. It is about 4 km southeast of **Sakhain village**, near Saipung in East Jaintia Hills, and often sold as Meghalaya's "Blue Lagoon". In the monsoon a surface stream drops over the cliff onto water rising out of the cave (16 m wide, 8 m high). The pool's colour comes from the limestone and minerals. It is about 140 km (4–5 hrs) from Shillong.

## Why go
- The water colour: reviewers call it "unbelievable", and it is a deep cobalt to turquoise blue even in winter.
- Very few visitors; no stalls or development.
- **Teens:** a real forest trek, the cave mouth, and a (very cold) swim.
- **Adults:** only for those comfortable with a 2 hr round-trip trek on rough ground and a steep climb back.

## Season reality
Dry season, so the road and trail are at their best and the pool is clearest. But the waterfall itself may not be flowing at all: the cave survey calls it "seasonally active", and a December reviewer found no falls. Expect a blue pool at a cave mouth rather than a waterfall. The water is very cold. The day is short, and the round trip from Jowai or Shillong is long, so start early.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** about 45–60 min each way through dense forest (one reviewer: "about an hour one way if you don't have a 4x4"). Plan about 2 hrs of walking round trip. Exact distance not published.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** rocky, narrow forest paths; the final descent to the base is on makeshift wooden steps made of tree branches. No railings.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** the branch steps and the rocks around the pool.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Only in the vehicle or in Sakhain village; there are no facilities at the trailhead.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** NH-6 east via Khliehriat towards Saipung and Sakhain. The final stretch is a bad road; a local 4x4 gets you closer to the trailhead. A tempo traveller probably cannot make it; confirm locally.

## Activities
- Forest trek with a local guide
- Swim in the blue pool (cold; no life jackets or lifeguards)
- Look into the Krem Labon cave mouth (exploring inside is for cavers only)

## Tips
- **Hire a local guide** in Saipung or Sakhain; the trail has no clear markings.
- Trekking shoes with grip, water, snacks and a change of clothes. Nothing is sold there.
- Download offline maps; mobile signal is likely to be poor.
- Leave from a Khliehriat or Jowai stay rather than Shillong to cut the driving.
- Best time, as first written: Start the trek by 10 am; be back at the road by 3:30 pm

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Krem Chympe Falls]] is about 13 km south (straight line) on the same remote side of East Jaintia Hills; both need guides and local 4x4s.
- [[Moopun Falls]] is about 24 km northwest; tours sell Labon + Moopun as one trip from Saipung.
- Khliehriat is the nearest town on NH-6.
