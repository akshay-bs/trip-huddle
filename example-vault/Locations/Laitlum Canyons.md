# Laitlum Canyons

## Overview
Laitlum ("end of the hills") is a grassy ridge on the edge of a deep, forested gorge. It is about 20–25 km south-east of Shillong via Smit, roughly 45 min–1 hr by road. From the rim you get a near-270° view over the canyons, with Rasong village far below.

## Why go
- One of the most dramatic big-landscape views near Shillong, reached with almost no effort.
- Adults can enjoy an easy wander along open grassy ridges with huge views. Teens get cliff-edge photos, wide-open space to roam, and the "Rock On 2" filming-location angle.

## Season reality
- The dry season brings good visibility. Guides consistently say afternoons are clearest, because fog often sits in the canyon in the morning and returns by evening.
- **Sunset is around 4:37 PM**. After about 3:30 PM the ridge gets cold and windy, so carry a jacket.
- Grass is golden-brown rather than monsoon-green, and the waterfalls on the far walls are thin or dry.
- Weekend and Christmas-week crowds are moderate, not overwhelming. The ridge is big enough to spread out.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** Two very different options:
  - **Rim / viewpoint walk (recommended):** the first viewpoint is a couple of minutes from parking. Wandering along the grassy ridge is about 1 km and 30–45 min round trip, at your own pace, and can be as short as you like.
  - **Stairway to Rasong village (skip):** a steep stairway of about **3,000 steps** down into the canyon. A round trip takes around 4–5 hours, and the climb back up is hard. It is **not suitable for this group's low-trek preference**. At most, teens could go down the first few dozen steps for a photo and come back.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** grass and dirt paths on the ridge, with a few rocky patches. Railings exist only at some points, and most of the rim is unfenced.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** **unfenced cliff edges** with sheer drops. Keep teens back from the edge, especially in wind or fog. The grass can be slippery with dew.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. The first viewpoint is almost next to the parking, so anyone can stop there.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** paved road via Smit to the parking at the top. A tempo traveller is fine.

## Activities
- Rim walk and viewpoint hopping along the ridge
- Photography: late-afternoon side light is lovely
- Picnic or tea at the top
- (Optional, not recommended for this group) the stairway trek to Rasong village

## Tips
- Arrive by about **2:30 PM** to get 1.5–2 hrs of light. The itineraries' "afternoon" slot fits, as long as it does not slip past 3:30 PM.
- If it is fogged in, wait 20–30 min, because the fog moves quickly.
- Carry water and snacks. Facilities are minimal, and toilets are basic or absent.
- Wear shoes with grip. Sneakers are fine for the rim.
- Best time, as first written: Early to mid afternoon (fog usually lifted; aim to arrive by 2:30 PM)

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Nongjrong Viewpoint]]: same south-east side of Shillong. Nongjrong at sunrise followed by Laitlum in the afternoon is a sensible pairing.
- [[Shillong Peak]]: another big-view stop near Shillong, though on a different road.
- Doing both plus Nongjrong in one day is unrealistic.
