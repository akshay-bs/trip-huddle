# Lyngksiar Falls

> [!info] One river, many falls
> [[Mawsawa Falls]], [[Janailar Falls]], [[Lyngksiar Falls]], [[Dainthlen Falls]] and [[Wei Sawdong Falls]] are all on the same stream system, west of the Shillong–Sohra road near Mawkma / Laitlyndop. Pick 2–3 easy ones rather than all.

## Overview
A three-tier waterfall near Mawkma, west of Sohra, with a swimming pool at the base. More developed than its neighbours: a paved trail, washrooms at the entrance and life jackets for hire.

## Why go
- One of the easiest "real" waterfalls in the chain, with a proper trail.
- **Teens:** swimming in the roped-off pool with a life jacket. **Adults:** the tiers and the well-kept path. Fossils are visible in the rocks (don't touch or remove them).

## Season reality
Flow is lower, but the pool is clear. Swimming is allowed with a life jacket; the water is cold.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** about 2 km round trip on a mild, paved trail, with some steps downhill in the last part (so a climb back).
- **Surface / steps / railings:** paved, well maintained.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** slippery floors and rocks near the pool.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at the entrance.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** road to the entrance; confirm big-vehicle parking.

## Activities
- Swimming in the roped area (life jacket for hire)
- Photos of the three tiers

## Tips
- Entry ₹50. Open 7 am – 6 pm per Polo Hotels, but Google Maps shows a 9:30 am opening; confirm before planning an early start. Washrooms at the entrance.
- Swimmers: carry a towel and warm layers.
- Wear shoes with grip.
- Best time, as first written: Late morning to midday
