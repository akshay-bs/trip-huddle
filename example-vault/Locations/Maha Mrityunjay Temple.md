# Maha Mrityunjay Temple

## Overview
A new Shiva temple at Puranigudam, about 12 km from Nagaon town in Assam. It was completed in 2021 and is built in the shape of a **126 ft Shivalinga**, promoted as the world's tallest. It is on the Guwahati → Kaziranga highway, about 2.5–3 hr from Guwahati.

## Why go
- An easy, striking stop that breaks the 5–6 hr drive to Kaziranga.
- **Adults:** darshan and the scale of the building. **Teens:** the size and the photos.

## Season reality
Dry, pleasant weather; no seasonal issues. Only useful if the route goes to Kaziranga: it isn't on the way to Pobitora or Meghalaya.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** short, from the parking to the temple (a few minutes).
- **Surface / steps / railings:** paved temple complex; some steps.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** just off the highway; big vehicles are fine.

## Activities
- Darshan; walk around the complex

## Tips
- Dress modestly; shoes off inside. Entry is free (donations welcome). Confirm the timings locally.
- Keep it to 30–45 min to reach Kaziranga before dark.

## Nearby / combine with
- On the way to [[Kaziranga National Park]] and Airavat Hotel
