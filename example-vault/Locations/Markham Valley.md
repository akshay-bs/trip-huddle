# Markham Valley

## Overview
Markham Valley is an offbeat landscape of rolling grassy hills and meadows near **Markham village in Eastern West Khasi Hills district**. This is **west of Shillong, not near Laitlum**. It is about 58–67 km from Shillong via Mairang and Shyrwang, typically 2–2.5 hrs by road. The main lookout is known as **Dommurok** (or "Laitmurok") **View Point**. The district's official portal calls the area "Markham and Remkhla Valley", lying between Markham and Mawkyllei villages. Travel blogs nickname it the "Mini Dzukou" of Meghalaya.

> [!warning] Location / planning doubt
> They are on **opposite sides of Shillong**: Laitlum is south-east via Smit, and Markham is 2–2.5 hrs west via Mairang. Combining both with a 3 AM Nongjrong sunrise in one day is not realistic for a 17-seater on hill roads. Most likely only one of the two will actually happen. Ask the operator which place they really mean.

## Why go
- Wide-open, uncrowded meadow scenery that is quite different from the gorges and waterfalls elsewhere on the trip. There are few tourists.
- Teens will like the space to run around and climb the grassy hillocks. Adults get a peaceful, "off the tourist circuit" countryside feel with views of Khasi villages.

## December reality
- The dry season brings clear visibility and pleasant days, with cold early mornings.
- The famous photos show **green** meadows (monsoon to autumn). By late December the grass is mostly **golden-brown**, which is still pretty but different.
- The "sea of clouds" and misty-meadow effect is a **sunrise** thing.
- Crowds stay low even in Christmas week. The main problem is distance and the rough last stretch, not people.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** short walk from where vehicles stop to Dommurok viewpoint. The exact distance was not documented. You can then wander the grassy hills as much or as little as you like. Budget 30–45 min round trip.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** natural grass and dirt; no built steps or railings reported.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** grass slopes can be slippery with dew or after rain. No major cliff exposure was reported.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. The viewpoint area is near the road, and anyone can stay by the vehicle.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** NH-106 to Shyrwang is fine. The final 10–15 km village road is narrow, with gravel, stone and mud patches and poor signage (Shillong Times, Oct 2025). Tour operators do sell tempo-traveller trips, but **confirm with the driver** that a 17-seater can do the last stretch.

## Activities
- Viewpoint photos at Dommurok / Laitmurok
- Easy meadow walks and hillock climbs
- Sunrise and mist (only if you stay nearby or leave Shillong very early)
- Village homestay stay (a couple exist in Markham)

## Tips
- If you keep it, give it **its own half-day** (or combine it with other West Khasi Hills spots) rather than squeezing it in after Nongjrong.
- Download offline maps. Signage is poor, and the drivers from Shillong may not know the village road.
- Carry snacks and water. There are only small shops.
- **Coordinates are moderately uncertain.** They come from the map pin on the official district tourism page, not a field-verified GPS point. Double-check in Google Maps ("Dommurok View Point" / "Markham Valley") before the trip.

## Nearby / combine with
- None of this trip's other locations are close. Everything else is east or south of Shillong.
