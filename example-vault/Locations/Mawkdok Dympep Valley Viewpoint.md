# Mawkdok Dympep Valley Viewpoint

## Overview
A roadside viewpoint on the Shillong–Sohra (Cherrapunji) road, about 30 km from Shillong and 24 km before Sohra. It looks into a deep, V-shaped green gorge, and many travellers treat it as the "gateway" to the Sohra area. A viewing platform and the Duwan Sing Syiem bridge were built by the Forest Department.

## Why go
- It is a natural, easy break on the Shillong→Sohra drive, with a big view for almost no effort.
- **Teens:** the **zipline** across the valley, run by Pioneer Adventure Tours and billed as one of the longest in the North-East, is the big draw.
- **Adults:** views, tea at the café, and photos in rented traditional Khasi dress.

## December reality
- Dry-season visibility is generally good. There can be mist in the gorge early in the morning, which usually lifts by mid-morning and adds drama rather than blocking the view.
- The valley is less lush than in the monsoon, but it is still green (forested).
- The zipline operates in the dry season. Weather or wind can pause it, so confirm on the day.
- It is on the main tourist road to Sohra, so expect many tour vehicles and a zipline queue around the Christmas week.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** steps down from the road to the viewing platform and bridge, and back up. About 0.3 km and roughly 15 min round trip. The exact step count was not found; it is described as "a number of steps".
- **Surface / steps / railings:** built concrete/stone steps and a platform with railings.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** steps can be damp from mist. The platform is railed.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. The view from the roadside and café is already good.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** directly on the main Shillong–Sohra road with roadside parking. Tempo traveller OK.

## Activities
- **Ziplining** with Pioneer Adventure Tours (pioneeradventuretour.com; +91 98560 06437):
  - **Small line:** 1,089 ft, **₹1,000** per person
  - **Big line:** 2,600 ft across the valley, **₹1,500** per person
  - These are the operator's current website prices. Older blogs and reviews quote ₹300–800, and discount sites offer lower prices, so confirm on the day.
  - **Age, height and weight limits: not published by the operator.** A third-party blog says minimum age 8, minimum height 120 cm, and maximum weight "typically 80–100 kg". Treat this as unverified and **call ahead**, especially for heavier adults. The 14- and 16-year-olds should be fine on age.
  - Allow about 45–75 min for a big group including briefing and queue. The big line is the thrill option.
- Viewpoint and bridge photos
- Traditional Khasi costume photo rental
- Tea or Maggi at the café, and shopping for shawls, caps and wooden souvenirs

## Tips
- Stop here **in the morning on the way to Sohra**, before the tour-bus crowd builds.
- If several people want the zipline, phone Pioneer the day before to check it is running and whether there is a group price.
- Carry cash for the zipline and stalls, and check whether UPI works on the day.
- The entry fee was not verified. It is generally reported as free or nominal, plus parking.
