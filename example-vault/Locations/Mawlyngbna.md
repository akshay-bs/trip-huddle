# Mawlyngbna

## Overview
A Khasi village on a plateau edge above the Bangladesh plains, about 15 km beyond Mawsynram and 75–80 km from Shillong. It sits on what was once a seabed, and 200-million-year-old fossils of sea urchins, shells and other marine animals show up in the rocks around it. Village youth run community adventure tourism here, based around [[Umkhakoi Lake]].

## Why go
- **Community tourism:** Mawlyngbna Traveller's Nest was started in 2014 by the Mawlyngbna Multipurpose Co-operative Society (about 240 members) to create jobs for local youth. It runs a campsite, a couple of cottages and guest houses, and trained local guides. Activities are booked through them, and a guide is required for every activity.
- **For teens:** river canyoning (wading, swimming, sliding down rock chutes, optional cliff jumps up to ~30 ft), kayaking, cliff jumping or rappelling at the lake.
- **For adults:** kayaking or rowing on Umkhakoi Lake, a short fossil or village walk, and the Split Rock crevice (Mawsiangjroi), a boulder split by the 1897 earthquake that you walk through, with ladders. The crevice suits most reasonably fit adults. Skip the 3-hr fossil trek and the full canyoning.
- **"Fossil park":** fossils are visible in rock across the village and around the lake. The longer Iew Luri Lura trail (about 3 hrs, canopy walk → Umdingkain waterfall → Pitcher Plant Park) passes preserved "footprint" fossils. There is also a Pitcher Plant Park with carnivorous plants.

## Season reality
Dry season (Oct–Apr) is the recommended time: dry rock, low and safe streams, clear skies over the plains. The catch is **cold water**. No temperature data is published, but late-December mountain stream and reservoir water will be cold (likely well below comfortable swimming temperature). Wetsuits are not mentioned by any source, so expect only life jackets. Kayaking and boating are fine; canyoning, cliff jumping and snorkelling will be bracing and short. Plan them for midday sun. Also note that the canyoning is most dramatic in monsoon/post-monsoon; in December the stream is lower and gentler, which is safer but less exciting. It is off the Christmas-week crowd circuit, but it is a Christian village: expect less activity on Sundays and 24–25 Dec.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** depends on the activity. Split Rock is about 40–60 min round trip; the short guided trek is about 2 hrs; the fossil trail is about 3 hrs; canyoning is about 2 hrs in the water. The lake itself needs only a short walk.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** forest trails, rock slabs, ladders in the crevice, stream beds. Few or no railings.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** wet rock in the canyon and at the lake edges; the narrow crevice with ladders; cliff-jump points (optional).
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. The lake and the Traveller's Nest area make an easy base for anyone sitting out.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** the Mawsynram road is paved. The final stretch into the village is narrow but motorable; a tempo traveller should manage (confirm with the driver).

## Activities
- River canyoning (about 2 hrs; teens and fit adults who can swim)
- Cliff jumping and rappelling (optional, teens)
- Kayaking and rowing on [[Umkhakoi Lake]] (everyone)
- Split Rock / Mawsiangjroi crevice walk (about 40–60 min; most adults)
- Fossil spotting around the lake and village; full Iew Luri Lura fossil trek (about 3 hrs; not for this group)
- Pitcher Plant Park, village walk, angling
- "Freshwater snorkelling"

## Tips
- **Accommodation for a big group:** Traveller's Nest is the main (practically the only) stay: tents (about ₹800/night in older reports), a couple of guest houses and cottages, with newer cottages costing more. Capacity is small. Rooms for a big group are likely only possible by booking the whole place plus tents, or splitting across Traveller's Nest and Gorgeview-type cottages. Book months ahead and confirm beds and bathrooms. It is basic, not a resort.
- Carry cash (no ATMs; nearest in Mawsynram), quick-dry clothes, towels, a warm layer and water shoes.
- Adults with knee or back issues should stick to kayaking and the short crevice walk.
- Contact numbers on mawlyngbna.com: +91 85753 79583 / +91 84138 70338 (WhatsApp). Verify before relying on them.
- Best time, as first written: Walks and treks in the morning; water activities around midday/early afternoon (warmest)

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Umkhakoi Lake]] is the activity hub for the village; do both together.
