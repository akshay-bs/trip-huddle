# Mawphlang Sacred Forest

## Overview
An ancient sacred grove of about 78 hectares at Mawphlang village, about 25 km south-west of Shillong. For centuries the Khasi community has protected it: nothing, not even a leaf or twig, may be taken out. Inside are huge moss-covered trees, orchids, ferns and old monoliths used for rituals. The **Khasi Heritage Village**, built by the Khasi Hills Autonomous District Council, is just opposite, with traditionally built huts of different Khasi house styles. The coordinates are OpenStreetMap's pin for the grove (Lawkyntang).

## Why go
- One of the most atmospheric places near Shillong: dense, quiet, primeval forest with a guide telling the Khasi legends and rules.
- **Teens:** the stories (the forest deity, the "don't take anything out" curse), giant roots, monoliths.
- **Adults:** an easy, flat-ish walk with real cultural depth; the heritage village adds architecture and lifestyle context.
- It's also the starting point of the [[David Scott Trail]].

## Season reality
A good time to visit: dry paths, clear skies, crisp air. Mawphlang is higher and colder than Shillong, and mornings can be misty. Closes about 4:30 pm, so don't leave it until after [[Laitlum Canyons]] late in the day. Laitlum is on the other side of Shillong, so doing both is a long day.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** guided loop. Short trail about 30–40 min; long trail about 90 min (3–4 km). Pick the short one.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** natural forest path with roots, stones and leaf litter; no railings.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** roots and moss can be slippery if damp. Wear shoes with grip. Nothing exposed.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. Anyone can wait at the heritage village or the entrance stalls.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** good paved road from Shillong; parking at the entrance.

## Activities
- Guided walk in the sacred forest (short or long loop)
- Khasi Heritage Village: traditional house styles, photos
- Monolith sites at the forest edge

## Tips
- **Take nothing out of the forest**, not even a leaf or pebble. The guide will remind you.
- Book guides at the entrance counter; with a big group, ask whether one guide can take the whole group.
- Carry cash for fees. Fee amounts come from 2024–25 blogs and may have gone up.
- Leave Shillong by about 9–9:30 am so the forest walk is done by early afternoon.
- Best time, as first written: Late morning to early afternoon

## Nearby / combine with
