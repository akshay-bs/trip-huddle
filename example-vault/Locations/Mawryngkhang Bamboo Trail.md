# Mawryngkhang Bamboo Trail

> [!question] Is this the "Bamboo Trek"?
> The trek usually sold under that name is this one at Wahkhen.

## Overview
A trail from Wahkhen village, East Khasi Hills, along hand-built bamboo walkways lashed to the cliffs above the Wahrew River, ending at the sacred U Mawryngkhang rock ("King of Stones") and the Mawmoit viewpoint. The walkways are built and maintained by the village.

## Why go
- **Teens:** swaying bamboo bridges on a cliff face, the most "adventure" walk in the region.
- **Adults:** the gorge scenery and the Khasi legend of the rock. But it is a real trek, not a stroll.

## Season reality
Dry season, so the bamboo is less slippery and the river is low and clear. Short days mean starting by about 9 AM to finish well before the 4:30 PM sunset, which is hard to combine with Mawkdok and the 90 km drive on the same day.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** about 3.5 km round trip (some sources say a 7 km loop), 4–5 hrs including the rock climb.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** bamboo walkways and ladders, forest paths, stream crossings. Rope handrails only.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** yes. Cliff-edge sections with drops beside the walkway.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at the village, but there is little to do there.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** village road; tempo traveller access not confirmed.

## Activities

## Tips
- Not a match for the group's "no long treks" rule. If kept, make it optional for the teens and keen adults with a guide.
- Easier alternatives with bamboo bridges: [[Garden of Caves]] and the zipline at [[Mawkdok Dympep Valley Viewpoint]].
- Best time, as first written: Start by 9 AM
