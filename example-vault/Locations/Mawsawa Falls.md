# Mawsawa Falls

> [!info] One river, many falls
> [[Mawsawa Falls]], [[Janailar Falls]], [[Lyngksiar Falls]], [[Dainthlen Falls]] and [[Wei Sawdong Falls]] are all on the same stream system, west of the Shillong–Sohra road near Mawkma / Laitlyndop. Pick 2–3 easy ones rather than all.

## Overview
A lesser-known, multi-tier waterfall with clear pools and forest around it, west of the Shillong–Sohra road. **Not the same as [[Seven Sisters Falls]]**, even though some websites mix them up (Seven Sisters is also called "Mawsmai Falls").

## Why go
- Quiet, uncrowded, and a short walk from the road. Blue pools for photos.
- **Teens:** pools and rocks. **Adults:** an easy walk to a pretty fall.

## Season reality
Lower flow, but the pools are at their clearest and the trail is dry. October–April is the recommended season.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** 5–10 min downhill from a small gate at the parking to the base (so a short climb back up).
- **Surface / steps / railings:** a rough forest trail with some steps.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** wet rocks by the pools.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at the parking.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** local road off the Sohra area; confirm that a big vehicle can reach the parking.
