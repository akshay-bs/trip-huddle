# Mawsmai Cave

## Overview
A limestone cave at Mawsmai village, about 6 km south of Sohra (Cherrapunji) town. It is the most visited and most accessible cave in Meghalaya. About 150 m of the system is open and lit, on a one-way route: you enter at one mouth and come out at another.

## Why go
- Real stalactites and stalagmites and water-sculpted rock, and you actually squeeze through the rock, which teens love.
- Short and lit, so it gives a caving taste with no gear and no guide needed.
- Easy to pair with [[Seven Sisters Falls]] and the Mawsmai side of Sohra in one stop.

## Season reality
December is a good time: the passages are drier, with less dripping water and mud than in the monsoon. The catch is crowds. Mawsmai is on every package itinerary, and in Christmas week the narrow one-way passage can back up into slow queues of 15–30 min at the tight spots. Go at opening time.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** Short walk (a few minutes) from parking past the stalls to the entrance, then about 150 m through the cave on a one-way route, then back to the parking area. About 0.5 km and 30–45 min in total, longer if there are queues.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** Some concrete steps and handrails at the entry and exit. Inside, uneven, polished limestone with a few steps up and down. The lighting is good but dim in places.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** The rock is polished smooth by visitors and can be slippery when damp. There are two or three **tight squeezes and low sections** where adults have to stoop, turn sideways or crouch briefly. Not good for anyone claustrophobic, broad-built or with bad knees or backs.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. Non-cavers can wait at the stalls near the parking area. Because the route is one-way, you cannot turn back easily once deep inside, so decide at the entrance.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** Paved road; the tempo traveller can reach the parking area.

## Activities
- Walk and squeeze through the lit one-way cave route
- Look at stalactites, stalagmites and fossil impressions
- Browse the stalls outside (snacks, local produce)

## Tips
- Arrive at opening time; queues build after about 10:30 am, especially 24–31 Dec.
- Wear shoes with grip and clothes you do not mind getting a little dirty. Leave big bags in the vehicle.
- Keep phones on a wrist strap or in a zipped pocket in the squeezes.
- Brief the group beforehand that it is one-way with tight spots, so anyone unsure opts out at the entrance.
- If the group prefers fewer crowds and more space, [[Arwah Cave]] is the calmer alternative. Doing both is usually overkill.
- Best time, as first written: Right at opening (about 9 am), before tour buses

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Seven Sisters Falls]]: the viewpoint is minutes away on the same road.
- [[Nohkalikai Falls]] on the same Sohra day.
- [[Arwah Cave]] as an alternative cave.
