# Mawsmai Nongthymmai Eco Park

## Overview
A government-run park on the plateau edge at Mawsmai, south of Sohra (Cherrapunji). It sits at the top of [[Seven Sisters Falls]], with the streams that feed the falls running through it. Paved paths, small bridges, a greenhouse with orchids and viewing points look over the gorge and on clear days across the Bangladesh plains. Coordinates come from its Google plus code (6PVQ+Q6Q), so they are approximate (about ±50 m).

## Why go
- Easy walking with big views over the edge of the Khasi Hills and down to Bangladesh.
- **Teens:** stream-side paths, rock perches and the "edge of the plateau" views.
- **Adults:** a relaxed, flat stop between the caves and falls.

## Season reality
Views are good on clear days, but the streams and the falls below are low in the dry season. Morning fog is common in Sohra, so go late morning or early afternoon. Worth it only because it's on the same road as [[Mawsmai Cave]] and [[Seven Sisters Falls]].

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** gentle loop on paved paths, about 1 km.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** paved; railings at the main viewpoints.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** wet rocks by the streams; the cliff edge beyond the railings is dangerous. Keep the teens behind the barriers.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, near the gate stalls.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** paved road; parking at the gate.

## Activities
- Walk the stream-side paths and viewpoints
- Look out over the Bangladesh plains
- Orchid greenhouse

## Tips
- Combine with the Seven Sisters Falls viewpoint and Mawsmai Cave in one stop cluster; they are all within a couple of km.
- The fee is small (₹20 per person reported); carry cash.

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Seven Sisters Falls]]: right below the park
- [[Mawsmai Cave]]: about 1 km
- [[Nohkalikai Falls]] and [[Mawkdok Dympep Valley Viewpoint]]: rest of the Cherrapunji day
