# Moopun Falls

## Overview
Known locally as the "waterfall with a beach". It is on the Umjhai River at Mutong village in East Jaintia Hills, about 25 km from both Jowai and Khliehriat and roughly 90 km (about 2.5–3 hrs) from Shillong. The main drop is about 110 ft high and 50 ft wide and lands on a sandy bank at the base, which is rare for Meghalaya. The river then flows on to the Myntdu.

## Why go
- **The beach:** a sandy bank at the foot of the falls, which is unusual in a state of rocky gorges. It is a good place to sit and picnic.
- **Two tiers:** at the upper tier, a natural **stone bridge** (about 33 ft long) spans the stream. Below it, a 20 ft drop forms a natural pool about 75 × 50 ft that looks like an infinity pool.
- **Pitcher plants** grow on the rocks around the falls.
- **Teens:** the sand, the stone bridge, and paddling or a quick swim in the pool.
- **Adults:** one of the easiest falls to reach in Meghalaya (5 min walk), with few tourists.

## Season reality
The East Jaintia Hills district names winter as the best time to visit. Flow is lower than in Oct–Nov, but reviews say the sandy bank is exposed after the rains, so December is when the beach shows best. The pool is clear and calm, but the water is cold, so swims will be short. It is well off the main tourist circuit, so expect few people even in Christmas week. Flow drops further by March. The valley loses sun early; leave by about 3:30 pm.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** about 5 min from the parking area down to the base of the falls, and 3 min to the stone bridge. Plan about 15 min of walking round trip.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** a well-kept path, a little steep in places. No step count published.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** wet rocks around the pool and on the stone bridge.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at the parking area, though the walk is short enough for almost everyone.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** turn off NH-6 (Shillong–Jowai–Silchar highway) at Kongong junction. From there it is about 10 km on a paved rural road via Pynthorsale and Mukhaialong. The parking area is about 300 m from the top of the falls. Highway signboards are poor; ask for "Moopun, Mutong".

## Activities
- Sit on the sandy bank at the base of the main falls
- Walk across the natural stone bridge
- Paddle or swim in the natural pool below the bridge (cold in December; no life jackets or lifeguards reported)
- Picnic. Carry food, as nothing is sold at the falls
- Spot pitcher plants on the rocks

## Tips
- Carry cash in case an entry or parking fee is charged.
- Bring a towel and a warm layer if anyone plans to swim; no changing rooms are reported.
- Sports shoes with grip for the path and the rocks.
- Download offline maps; the turn-off from the highway is easy to miss.
- Best time, as first written: Late morning to early afternoon (sun on the pool and the sand)

## Nearby / combine with
- **Jowai** is 25 km away. It fits a Jowai day with [[Nartiang Monoliths]], or a return to Shillong via Jowai.
- [[Krang Suri Falls]] and [[Phe Phe Falls]] are south of Jowai on the Amlarem road. Moopun is southeast of Jowai on NH-6, the other direction, so doing it the same day adds about 50 km (1.5–2 hrs) round trip from Jowai.
- [[Krem Chympe Falls]] is further east on the same NH-6 corridor (near Lumshnong); the two could share a day from a Khliehriat stay.
- [[Labon Falls]] (the East Jaintia "Blue Lagoon") is about 24 km southeast near Saipung; tours sell Labon + Moopun together, but Labon needs a 2 hr forest trek and a 4x4.
- Umhang Lake (Bataw) is about 30 km away; the "Dem Khla" viewpoint is about 500 m from the falls.
