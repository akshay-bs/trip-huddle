# Nartiang Monoliths

## Overview
The largest collection of monoliths (megaliths) in Meghalaya, at Nartiang village in West Jaintia Hills. It has hundreds of upright stones (menhirs) and flat table stones (dolmens), put up by the Jaintia kings from about the 14th–17th century. The tallest is about 8 m. A short walk away is the **Nartiang Durga Temple**, one of the 51 Shakti Peethas, on the site of an older temple where human sacrifice took place until British rule.

## Why go
- Something very different from waterfalls and viewpoints: a "garden" of standing stones.
- **Teens:** the scale of the stones and the stories. **Adults:** history, and the Durga temple.

## December reality
Dry and clear. No seasonal problems. Shillong to Nartiang is about 65 km (about 2–2.5 hr); Jowai to Nartiang is 24 km (about 40 min).

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** flat walk around the monolith site (about 20–30 min), plus a short walk to the Durga temple.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** grass and paths; flat.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** village road off the Jowai area; any vehicle.
