# Navagraha Temple

## Overview
A temple to the nine planets (Navagraha) on top of Chitrachal Hill in east-central Guwahati. It was built in the late 18th century by the Ahom king Rajeswar Singha. Inside the dome-shaped sanctum are nine Shiva lingas, each representing one celestial body. It was historically linked with astronomy and astrology, which gave Guwahati its old name *Pragjyotishpura* ("city of eastern astronomy").

## Why go
- A short, easy stop with a unique theme and history.
- **Adults:** religious significance and the astronomy connection.
- **Teens:** the hilltop and the story; otherwise a quick visit.
- City views from the hill.

## Season reality
No seasonal issues. Pleasant weather for the short climb.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** short uphill walk from where vehicles stop to the hilltop temple.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** paved path and steps.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none significant.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at the parking area.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** narrow hill road; the tempo traveller may need to stop lower down.

- Darshan of the nine lingas
- City views from Chitrachal Hill

## Tips
- Free entry. Dress modestly and remove shoes.
- Fits between [[Assam State Museum]] and the Kalakshetra on the city-tour loop.
- Best time, as first written: Mid-morning
