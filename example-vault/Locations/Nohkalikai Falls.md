# Nohkalikai Falls

## Overview
India's tallest plunge waterfall. It drops about 340 m (1,115 ft) off the Sohra plateau into a green-blue plunge pool, about 5 km west of Sohra (Cherrapunji) town. You view it from a railed viewpoint complex at the top of the gorge.
- Coordinates above are for the **viewpoint** (OpenStreetMap "Nohkalikai Falls View Point"). The falls themselves are at about 25.2757, 91.6866 (Wikipedia/OSM), about 500 m across the gorge.

## Why go
- A classic Meghalaya sight: a huge cliff, a single ribbon of water, and a coloured pool far below. It is also linked to a well-known Khasi legend (Ka Likai).
- Almost no walking needed, so the whole group can go.
- Teens: dramatic scale and photos. Adults: the view plus local spice and honey shopping at the stalls.

## Season reality
- **Flow:** In the dry season (Dec–Feb) the falls lose most of their power. Expect a thin white ribbon, not a torrent. The plunge pool is at its most vivid colour in winter, and the gorge is still spectacular.
- **Fog:** Sohra often has fog or cloud. On a bad day the gorge is a white wall for 15–60 min and then clears briefly. Late morning to early afternoon is usually the best bet; be ready to wait.
- **Crowds:** One of the busiest stops in Sohra. Very crowded 24 Dec – 1 Jan, with parking jams around midday.
- It is still worth it in December for the scale, but set expectations: this is not the monsoon photo.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** Short paved walk with some steps from the parking area through the stall area to the viewing platforms. About 5–10 min each way. Several viewing decks are spread along the rim.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** Paved path and concrete steps with railings at the viewing decks.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** None on the viewpoint route. **The trail to the pool at the bottom** (starting near the "Top of Nohkalikai Falls" trailhead) is a long, steep trek: about 3,000–3,500 steps and rough path, 2 hrs down and more to come back, 4–5 hrs total. **Skip it for this group.**
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. The stalls and cafe area are right at the parking area.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** Good paved road; the tempo traveller can reach the parking lot.

## Activities
- View the falls from the main and side viewing decks
- Hear the Ka Likai legend (signboards or local guides)
- Buy local cinnamon, bay leaves, black pepper and honey at the stalls
- Tea or snacks with the gorge view

## Tips
- If it is fogged in, have tea and wait 20–30 min. The cloud often lifts briefly.
- Go before noon in Christmas week to beat the parking queue.
- Carry cash for entry, camera, parking and toilets.
- Do not let anyone start down the bottom-pool trail casually. It is a half-day trek, not a stroll.
- Best time, as first written: Late morning to early afternoon (fog usually lifts; sun lights the pool)

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Wei Sawdong Falls]] and [[Arwah Cave]] are on the same (west) side of Sohra.
- [[Mawsmai Cave]] and [[Seven Sisters Falls]] are on the Mawsmai side, south of town, and usually done the same day.
