# Nongjrong Viewpoint

## Overview
A hilltop sunrise viewpoint above Nongjrong village (Mawkynrew block, East Khasi Hills), roughly 50–65 km from Shillong. The drive takes about 2–2.5 hours. On clear winter mornings the valleys below fill with a "sea of clouds" (a cloud inversion) and the sun rises over it. The Umngot river valley lies below.

## Why go
- One of the best sunrise-over-clouds spots in Meghalaya. It is still less commercial than Laitlum or Shillong Peak.
- Adults get the view and the quiet. Teens get a genuinely unusual sight and great photos, and can say they were above the clouds.
- A Khasi village breakfast afterwards is part of the experience.

## December reality
- **Sea of clouds:** December to February is the best window. Clear skies and cold, still nights make valley fog most likely. Guides call the chances "high", but it is weather-dependent. Some mornings the ridge itself is fogged in and you see nothing, and on others the sky is clear with no cloud layer. Build in a backup morning if this is a must.
- The itineraries' "5 AM arrival" is earlier than strictly needed. Being at the top by **5:30 AM** is enough to see the pre-dawn colours.
- **Cold:** Shillong December nights drop to about 4–7 °C, and an exposed ridge at dawn with wind feels colder. Expect roughly 3–8 °C. This is an estimate, as no local measurement was found. Bring thermals, a down jacket, cap and gloves.
- **Crowds:** Nongjrong now goes viral every winter.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** short uphill walk from where vehicles stop to the hilltop, about 10–15 min each way. The exact distance was not found. Budget 30 min round trip including photo stops.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** village path and grassy slope. The informal viewing area has no proper railings.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** you walk up in the dark, and grass is wet with dew. Carry torches or phone lights. The edges of the hilltop are open drops, so keep teens back from them.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. Anyone can wait in the vehicle or a homestay, but they would miss the point of the 3 AM start.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** the route is via the Mawkynrew side, and guides describe slightly different routes. The final stretch is on narrow village roads, reported as slightly rough. Groups do come in tempo travellers, but ask the driver whether a 17-seater can reach the upper parking.

## Activities
- Sunrise and sea-of-clouds viewing and photography
- Khasi breakfast in the village: tea, *pukhlein* and *putharo* (rice cakes), *jadoh* if available
- Optional for the energetic: Nongjrong Falls, about 3 km away through forest. This is a trek and **not suitable for this group**.

## Tips
- **Pre-book breakfast** for a big group with a homestay or the driver's contact. Stalls are informal, and nothing is guaranteed at 7 AM for a big group.
- Carry torches, flasks of hot tea or coffee, and snacks for the drive. There are **no toilets** at the top.
- The entry fee is unclear. A 2026 guide says ₹30 per person plus parking, while other blogs say there is no official fee but a contribution may be asked. Carry small cash.
- The mobile network may be weak, so download offline maps.
- If the morning is fully fogged in, wait until about 7:30 AM. The fog often breaks and the cloud layer appears below as the sun warms it.

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Laitlum Canyons]]: on the same side of Shillong (south-east, via Smit). It pairs naturally the same day, in the afternoon when Laitlum's fog has cleared.
- [[Umngot River (Dawki)]]: the Umngot flows in the valley below Nongjrong, but it is not a practical same-morning combination.
- [[Markham Valley]]: note it is on the **opposite (west) side** of Shillong, so it pairs poorly with Nongjrong on one day.
