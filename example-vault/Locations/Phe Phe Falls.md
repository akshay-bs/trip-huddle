# Phe Phe Falls

## Overview
A two-tiered waterfall with a turquoise plunge pool near Shkentalang village in the Amlarem area of West Jaintia Hills, about 20 km (20–30 min) south of Jowai and ~85–90 km from Shillong. It is in a bamboo-forested gorge and is reached from the Paradise Adventure Camp area by a river crossing and a stepped descent.

## Why go
- A quieter, less commercial alternative to the Sohra falls, with a clear blue-green pool at the bottom and two tiers visible from the base.
- **Teens:** the boat crossing, the bamboo-forest descent and scrambling on the rocks by the pool.
- **Adults:** a pretty setting, but only worth it for those happy to climb back up a few hundred steps.

## Season reality
The falls do not dry up, but in late December the flow is much thinner than in the monsoon, so expect a gentle two-tier cascade rather than a torrent. The pool is at its clearest and bluest, the steps are dry and less slippery, and it is pleasant and cool for walking. The water is cold, so swimming in December is only for the very brave (the chalbanjare guide says swimming is generally not recommended). It is off the main Christmas-week circuit, so crowds should be light. The gorge gets shade and dark early; finish by about 3:30 pm.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** from parking at Paradise Adventure Camp, cross the river by boat or kayak, walk 7–10 min across meadows to the entry point, then descend about 200–300 steps through bamboo groves. Sources give 15–20 min to the upper viewpoint and 25–30 min to the first viewpoint, then about 10 min more down to the pool. Plan about **30–40 min down and 40–45 min back up**, roughly 75–90 min of walking round trip. Exact distance not published.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** concrete/stone steps built around 2022 (it was a steep dirt path before); some bamboo railings. It is a moderately steep descent, and **the uphill return is the hard part.**
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** rocks around the pool; damp steps in shaded sections.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. They can wait at the campsite eatery by the river, or go only as far as the upper viewpoint (15–20 min).
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** paved road to the campsite and parking; a tempo traveller should manage (confirm locally). Do not rely on GPS alone; ask locals for "Paradise Adventure Camp / Phe Phe".

## Activities
- Boat or kayak across the river at the camp
- Walk down to the pool; photos of both tiers
- Swimming with a life jacket (cold in December; optional)
- Lunch at the campsite eatery

## Tips
- Sports shoes with grip; hands free for the railings.
- Carry water; nothing is sold at the bottom.
- Split the group: the fitter members do the full descent, the rest stay at the campsite or the first viewpoint.
- Anyone with knee problems should skip the full descent.
- Keep cash for the boat, life jackets and entry.
- Best time, as first written: Late morning to early afternoon (sun on the pool; back up well before 4 pm)

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Krang Suri Falls]] is about 8 km away in a straight line (further by road) in the same Amlarem area; If time or energy is short, choose Krang Suri (more famous, fewer boat logistics).
- [[Umngot River (Dawki)]] and [[Shnongpdeng]] are further south on the same side of the hills.
