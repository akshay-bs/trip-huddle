# Pobitora Wildlife Sanctuary

## Overview
Pobitora is a small (about 39 km²) grassland and wetland sanctuary on the south bank of the Brahmaputra in Morigaon district, about 45–50 km east of Guwahati. It has one of the highest densities of one-horned rhinos in the world and Assam's second-largest rhino population after Kaziranga. It is often called "mini Kaziranga".

## Why go
- Rhino sightings are close to certain because the park is small and open. It is the easiest rhino safari to fit into a Guwahati-based trip.
- Teens: an open jeep safari, rhinos and wild buffalo up close, and migratory birds on the beels (wetlands) in winter.
- Adults: a short, low-effort outing of about 1 hr in the jeep with almost no walking.

## Season reality
- **Season:** December is peak season. The park reopens after the monsoon, usually in October or November (the 2024-25 season opened on 14 Oct), and closes again around May or June.
- **Weather:** days are dry and pleasant. Mornings are cold (about 10–12 °C) and often foggy, so the first slot can be misty, which is atmospheric but hazy for photos. Carry jackets for the open jeep.
- **Grass:** some grassland is still tall in December because controlled burning usually happens Jan–Feb, but rhinos are regularly seen near the tracks.
- **Crowds:** only about 20 jeeps are allowed per slot, and Christmas-week slots sell out. Book ahead.
- **Verdict:** well worth it.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** minimal. It is a few minutes from the parking or range office to the jeep stand or elephant mount. Walking inside the sanctuary is not allowed because of the rhinos and wild buffalo.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** flat ground. The elephant ride uses a raised mounting platform with a few steps.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none of note. The dirt tracks are dusty in December.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, there is a waiting area at the range office.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** rural roads via Jagiroad or Mayong. The tempo traveller can reach the range office and parking.
- **Group logistics:** a big group need **3 jeeps** (max 6 per jeep).

## Activities
- **Jeep safari** (about 1–1.5 hrs, core area). Slots are roughly 7:00–8:30, 8:30–10:00 and 9:30–11:00 AM, then 1:00–2:30 and 2:00–3:30 PM, and some agents sell a full day. Max 6 per jeep. Fees vary a lot by source:
  - older blogs say about ₹2,000 per jeep, with entry (₹50 per Indian) and a guide (about ₹300) extra
  - online agents quote about ₹3,200–3,400 per jeep including permits
- **Elephant safari:** early-morning slots only (about 6–8 AM), 2- or 4-seater elephants, a limited number each day, about ₹800–1,700 per seat depending on source. It gets you closest to rhinos through the tall grass.
  - **Is it advisable?** It is optional. It is short and memorable, but the seats are scarce, it needs a very early start after arrival day, and elephant riding raises animal-welfare concerns that some families will want to avoid. The jeep safari alone is enough to see rhinos.
- **Birdwatching** on the beels (migratory ducks in winter).
- **"Nature walk"**: walking inside the park is not allowed. Treat this as a short stroll around the fringe villages or the Mayong area, or skip it.

## Tips
- Book jeeps in advance for Christmas week. Confirm the exact fee, slot and whether permits are included, because private agent sites and older blogs quote very different prices.
- On arrival day, aim for the 1:00 or 2:00 PM jeep slot. The morning slots need a 5:30 AM start from Guwahati.
- Wear layers and a cap. Open jeeps are cold and dusty in the morning.
- Stay seated and quiet near rhinos, and don't get out of the jeep.
- Carry cash. The network is patchy.
- Best time, as first written: First morning slot (about 7–8:30 AM) for animals and soft light. The afternoon slots (1–3 PM) suit a same-day arrival from Guwahati
