# Police Bazaar

## Overview
Shillong's main shopping and eating district, also called Khyndailad, centred on the Police Point junction. It is named after a police station built here in British times. It is a compact, walkable grid of shops, malls, cafés and street stalls in the heart of the city. Ward's Lake is a short walk away.

## Why go
- It is the easiest place to feel Shillong's buzz: evening lights, music, and street food.
- **Teens:** cheap winter wear, sneakers, Korean and Japanese cosmetics, momos, and cafés.
- **Adults:** handicrafts, local produce (Khasi honey, tea, pickles, bay leaf, cinnamon), and Khasi food such as jadoh.

## Season reality
This is the best time of year to visit. Shillong is strongly Christian, and the streets are decorated with Christmas lights and stars from early December. The week before Christmas is **very crowded**, with shoppers, traffic jams and full restaurants. It gets cold after sunset (roughly 5–8 °C), so wear warm layers.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** a relaxed loop of about 1–2 km around the Police Point, Police Bazar Road and GS Road area. How long you stay is up to you.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** paved streets and pavements, crowded and a bit uneven in places.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none. Watch for traffic and pickpockets in the crowds.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. People can split up and sit in a café, or head back to the hotel.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** a tempo traveller can drop the group at Police Point. Parking is hard to find, so fix a pickup time and place with the driver.

## Activities
- **Shopping:**
  - winter jackets, caps, boots and sneakers from street stalls and shops
  - bamboo and cane crafts, Khasi and Garo shawls and stoles
  - Tibetan jewellery (Glory's Plaza area)
  - local produce: Khasi honey, Meghalaya tea, Lakadong turmeric, pickles, dried fruit and spices
- **Food worth trying:**
  - **Trattoria:** a small Khasi eatery known for jadoh (rice cooked with pork) and pork dishes. It opens around lunchtime and sells out early.
  - **Delhi Mistan Bhandar:** jalebi, chaat and sweets, and an easy vegetarian option
  - **Eee Cee:** a restaurant and bakery at Police Point, good for puffs and quick meals
  - **Jiva Veg:** a pure-veg restaurant
  - **City Hut Dhaba:** nearby in Oakland, for family North Indian meals
  - Evening street momos, *tungrymbai* (fermented soybean) and *dohneiiong* (pork with black sesame) for the adventurous
- Evening walk under the Christmas lights

## Tips
- **Sunday:** many shops and eateries are shut or keep short hours. Plan the market for a weekday or Saturday.
- Go from about 4 pm and finish by 8 pm. After that the area empties quickly and street stalls pack up.
- Bargain at the street stalls. Fixed prices apply in the malls and branded shops.
- Carry some cash. Most shops take UPI, but small stalls may not.
- Keep the group together, or agree on a meeting point such as the Police Point junction. It gets very crowded before Christmas.
- The restaurants named here come from travel guides and reviews. Check current hours and ratings closer to the trip.

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Ward's Lake]]: about a 10-minute walk away. Do it earlier in the afternoon, since it closes around 5–5:30 pm in winter.
- [[Shillong Peak]] and [[Elephant Falls]]: do these earlier in the day in Upper Shillong, then finish here in the evening.
