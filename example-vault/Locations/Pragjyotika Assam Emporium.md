# Pragjyotika Assam Emporium

## Overview
The Assam government's handicraft and handloom showroom in central Guwahati, on GNB Road in Ambari next to Panbazar. It sells Assam silks (muga, pat and eri), mekhela chadors, gamosas, bamboo and cane work, brass and bell-metal ware and wooden crafts at fixed prices.

## Why go
- **Adults:** genuine Assam silk and bell-metal ware at government prices, with no bargaining and less risk of fakes than street stalls.
- **Teens:** small gifts (gamosas, japi hats, bamboo crafts).
- It is about 1 km from the Umananda ferry ghat, so it fits the last morning before the airport.

## Season reality

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** none beyond the shop.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** shop floor.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. Panbazar has cafés, and the riverfront is close by.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** drop-off on GNB Road. Parking is limited, so the driver may need to circle.

## Activities
- Shop for Assam silk (muga, pat and eri) stoles, sarees and mekhela chadors
- Gamosas, japi (bamboo hats), cane and bamboo crafts, bell-metal and brass ware

## Tips
- Check the opening hours a day ahead; they are not published online.
- Carry a card or UPI for big silk purchases, and ask for the silk-mark certificate on muga or pat silk.
- Best time, as first written: Late morning, after the Umananda ferry

## Nearby / combine with

- [[Assam State Museum]] and [[Navagraha Temple]] are in the same part of town
