# Prut Falls

> [!info] One river, many falls
> [[Mawsawa Falls]], [[Janailar Falls]], [[Lyngksiar Falls]], [[Dainthlen Falls]] and [[Wei Sawdong Falls]] are all on the same stream system, west of the Shillong–Sohra road near Mawkma / Laitlyndop. Pick 2–3 easy ones rather than all.

## Overview
A 40 m curtain waterfall on the Wah Urwan river at **Laitlyndop village** (Laitduh), west of Sohra, between [[Mawsawa Falls]] and [[Janailar Falls]]. The river is fed by streams from Khliehshnong Laitlyndop, Umkrem and Madan Umkrem. It opened to tourists on 26 Sept 2021; the village council built the footpath and viewpoint. It is still less visited than the big Sohra falls.

## Why go
- A thin curtain fall over limestone into a clear blue-green pool, and **a path that goes behind the waterfall**.
- **Teens:** walking behind the falls. **Adults:** a short paved walk and a quiet spot (4.8 on Google from about 800 reviews).

## Season reality
Lower flow, so a thinner curtain, but the pool is clear and the trail is dry. Walking behind the falls is easier and safer when the flow is low. Too cold to swim. Crowds are low, though it is getting more popular.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** sources give 15–20 min, about 20 min and 25–30 min each way from the car park, so about 50 min round trip. Exact distance not published.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** a paved footpath, described as easy, with downhill steps in the last stretch (a short climb back up). Railings unknown.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** wet rock on the path behind the falls and around the pool.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at the entrance (washrooms there).
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** road to the car park at Laitduh / Laitlyndop (Plus Code 8M7R+8MV); confirm big-vehicle parking with the driver.

## Activities
- Walk down to the pool; walk behind the falls on the path on the right-hand side
- Photos from the viewpoint

## Tips
- Entry ₹50. Open 7 am – 6 pm. Washrooms at the entrance. Carry cash.
- Shoes with grip for the wet rock behind the falls.
- Easy to pair with [[Janailar Falls]] (about 1 km east) and [[Lyngksiar Falls]] on the same morning.
- Best time, as first written: Late morning to midday

## Nearby / combine with
- The waterfall chain: [[Mawsawa Falls]], [[Lyngksiar Falls]], [[Janailar Falls]], [[Dainthlen Falls]]
- [[Arwah Cave]] and [[Nohkalikai Falls]] (Safar with Sasha does these the same day)
