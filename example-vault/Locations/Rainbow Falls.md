# Rainbow Falls

## Overview
An 80 ft waterfall in the forest beyond Nongriat village, below Sohra in East Khasi Hills. It drops into a deep, blue-green pool, and on sunny mornings the spray makes a rainbow, which gives the falls its name. There is no road: it is the far end of the Nongriat trek, about 3 km beyond the [[Double Decker Living Root Bridge]], which is itself 3,000–3,500 steps down from Tyrna.

## Why go
- One of the most beautiful falls in Meghalaya: a vivid blue pool, a rainbow in the spray and jungle all around.
- Far fewer people than at the Double Decker bridge, because most visitors turn back there.
- **Teens:** the full adventure trek, with suspension bridges, root bridges and a jungle walk.
- **Adults:** only for those fit enough for a 9-hour day of steps.

## Season reality
Good trekking season: dry paths, cool air and clear water. The flow is thinner than after the monsoon, but the rainbow still appears when the morning sun hits the spray. The problem is daylight. With sunset around 4:30 pm, the round trip from Tyrna (about 7–9 hrs) only works with a 6:30–7 am start and no long stops, or by staying a night at a Nongriat homestay.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** Tyrna → Double Decker bridge, 1.5–2 hrs down. Then the bridge → Rainbow Falls, about 3 km, typically 1–1.5 hrs each way (one source says up to 2–3 hrs). Then all the way back, including the 3,000–3,500-step climb to Tyrna. Plan about 7–9 hrs of walking round trip.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** stone steps and forest paths with more climbs and descents after Nongriat, a metal suspension bridge about 20 min past the Double Decker, and another root bridge.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** wet rocks at the pool and on stream crossings. The pool is deep with currents; swimming is not officially recommended.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes: trekkers can stop at the Double Decker bridge or the [[Blue Lagoon (Nongriat)]] and wait there, or not go down at all.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** paved road to Tyrna parking; tempo traveller OK.

## Activities
- See the rainbow in the spray (best around 10 am – noon on a sunny day)
- Sit by the blue pool; a dip only in calm, shallow spots and only if the guide says it is safe
- Cross the suspension bridge and the extra root bridge on the way

## Tips
- Not a match for the group's "no long treks" rule. If anyone goes, it should be the fittest teens and adults with a guide, ideally with a night at a Nongriat homestay so the climb is split.
- Leave Tyrna by 7 am at the latest, and turn back by 1 pm wherever you are.
- Carry water, snacks, a head torch and a warm layer; shops thin out after the Double Decker.
- Shoes with good grip.
- Best time, as first written: Reach the falls between about 10 am and noon for the rainbow; that means leaving Tyrna by 6:30–7 am

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Double Decker Living Root Bridge]] and [[Blue Lagoon (Nongriat)]] are on the same trail; all three are one trek.
- Stays at the Tyrna trailhead: Royal View Resort, Smoky Falls Mae Fi Resort.
