# Rangthylliang Living Root Bridge

## Overview
The "Rangthylliang 1" root bridge near Mawkyrnot village, Pynursla area, East Khasi Hills. At over 50 m (about 53 m by Meghalaya Tourism) it is described as the **longest known living root bridge**, about 30 m above the stream. It is grown from the roots of two rubber trees (*Ficus elastica*) on opposite banks. The trek starts at Rangthylliang and ends at Mawkyrnot, which has several more root bridges.

## Why go
- **Teens and keen adults:** the "world's longest" claim, a quiet forest trail, and far fewer tourists than Riwai or Nongriat.
- Meghalaya Tourism runs a guided walk with a "root bridge architect" that passes several bridges.

## Season reality
Dry, cool and clear: the best time for the steps. Daylight is the constraint.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** about 1,500 steps down and the same back up. Sources call the steps well built, but the return climb is the hard part. Allow about 3 hours.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** stone steps; railings unknown.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** the bridge is high above the stream; mossy steps near water.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. The trailhead is beside the highway and close to [[Conara Resort]], so non-trekkers can stay at the hotel.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** on the Pynursla–Dawki road; the tempo traveller can drop at the village.

## Activities
- Trek to the bridge and walk across it
- Guided Meghalaya Tourism walk past several root bridges

## Tips
- Treat it as optional: teens and keen adults with a local guide, early morning.
- [[Riwai Living Root Bridge]] is the easy alternative, about a 10-minute walk.
- Carry water and wear shoes with grip.

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Conara Resort]] (same village area), [[Mawlynnong Village]], [[Riwai Living Root Bridge]]
