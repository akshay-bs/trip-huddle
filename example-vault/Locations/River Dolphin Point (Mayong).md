# River Dolphin Point (Mayong)

> [!question] Which spot is meant?
> No signposted viewpoint by that name was found online (Oct 2026).

## Overview
The Ganges river dolphin (*xihu* or *sihu* in Assamese), Assam's state aquatic animal and an endangered species, lives in the Brahmaputra and its tributaries. Near Mayong and Pobitora, an unverified online account describes a spot about 1 km from Mayong village, at the Kopili–Brahmaputra confluence, where dolphins surface. A dolphin was also recorded at Garanga beel on Pobitora's boundary.

## Why go
- **Everyone:** a short, easy riverside stop and a chance to see a rare freshwater dolphin.
- Low effort, fits between the morning safari and the drive.

## December reality
After the monsoon (Sept–Oct) is best. By December the water is lower and sightings are less reliable. Treat it as a bonus, not a highlight. The better-known site, the Kulsi river at Kukurmara (about 40 km west of Guwahati), is in the wrong direction for this route.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** none to minimal (riverbank).
- **Surface / steps / railings:** natural bank.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** muddy banks near the water.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, in the vehicle.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** to be confirmed locally.
