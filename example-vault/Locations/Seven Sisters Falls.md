# Seven Sisters Falls

## Overview
Nohsngithiang Falls ("Seven Sisters", also called Mawsmai Falls) is a seven-segment waterfall about 1 km south of Mawsmai village, about 5 km from Sohra (Cherrapunji) town. It plunges about 315 m off a long limestone cliff. You see it from across the valley at a roadside viewpoint on the Sohra–Mawsmai road.
- Coordinates above are for the **viewpoint** (OpenStreetMap "Seven Sisters Waterfalls Viewpoint"). The falls on the cliff opposite are at about 25.2430, 91.7387–91.7416 (OSM; Wikipedia gives 25.243, 91.743).

## Why go
- In the monsoon it is one of India's tallest falls, with seven parallel streams along the cliff.
- In December the reason to stop is the **view**: the long cliff line and, on a clear day, the Bangladesh plains in the distance. It is a quick photo stop on the way to [[Mawsmai Cave]], not a destination.

## Season reality
Be honest with the group: **there is little or no waterfall to see in December.** Wikipedia notes it flows "only during the rainy season". Several Tripadvisor reviews from December visits are titled along the lines of "No water in December" and "Don't go in December". At best you may see one or two faint trickles on the cliff. Morning haze or fog can also hide the cliff. Treat it as a free roadside viewpoint, not a waterfall. Don't make a separate trip for it.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** Almost none. The viewpoint is next to the road, a few steps from where the vehicle stops.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** Level roadside area with barriers at the edge.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** None at the viewpoint; keep teens behind the barrier at the cliff edge.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, anyone can stay in the vehicle.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** Paved state road; the tempo traveller can pull over at the roadside parking. Parking can be tight when tour vehicles bunch up.

## Activities
- Photo stop at the roadside viewpoint
- Tea at the roadside stalls
- Optional: Mawsmai Nongthymmai Eco Park, near the top of the falls, a small park with walkways and valley views

## Tips
- Combine it with Mawsmai Cave: same road, 5–10 min apart.
- Stop on the way back only if the sky is clear; if it is foggy there is nothing to see.
- Show the teens the monsoon photo so they know what the cliff looks like in full flow.

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Mawsmai Cave]]: same road, minutes away.
- [[Nohkalikai Falls]]: the Sohra falls that still has visible (if thin) flow in December.
- [[Arwah Cave]] and [[Wei Sawdong Falls]] on the west side of Sohra.
