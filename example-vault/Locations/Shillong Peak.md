# Shillong Peak

## Overview
At about 1,965 m, this is the highest point around Shillong and in Meghalaya. It lies in the Laitkor area of Upper Shillong, about 10 km from the city centre. The viewpoint is inside an Indian Air Force radar station, so entry is controlled.

**On the name:** "Shillong Peak" and "Laitkor Peak" mean the same place for visitors. Google Maps lists it as *"Shillong View Point, Laitkor Peak"*. Laitkor is the locality, and the peak is named after U Shulong, the local deity.

## Why go
- A 360° view over Shillong city and the surrounding Khasi hills. On very clear days you can see as far as the Bangladesh plains.
- **Teens:** the telescope tower and photos of the city from above. Some vendors also rent traditional Khasi costumes for photos.
- **Adults:** an easy, low-effort viewpoint with almost no walking. It is a good first look at Meghalaya.

## Season reality
December is one of the best months for visibility because the air is dry and the skies are often clear. Still, fog and cloud can roll in at any time, and afternoon haze reduces the view. It is cold and windy at the top, so carry jackets. Christmas-week crowds are moderate because it is on the standard city-tour route. **Timing risk:** the gate reportedly closes around 3:30 pm. Check this when planning the day, or move the visit to a morning.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** a short walk (about 100–150 m each way) from the parking area to the viewing deck. There are a few steps up to the telescope tower.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** paved, with railings at the deck.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none significant. It is breezy at the edge.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, but the walk is so short that everyone can go.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** paved road through the IAF campus up to the parking. A tempo traveller can reach it. **No photography is allowed while driving through the base**, only at the tourist viewpoint.

## Activities
- Panoramic view and photos of Shillong city
- Telescope view (small fee, per person, timed)
- Khasi traditional costume photos (optional, vendor-run)
- Tea and snacks at the stalls

## Tips
- **Every person, including the teens, must carry an original photo ID** (Aadhaar or school ID for the teens). You will be checked at the IAF gate.
- Fees differ between sources. Some list ₹30 per person plus ₹30 per vehicle, while others say entry is free. Carry cash.
- Older guides say cameras must be deposited at the gate. Recent visitors say phones and cameras are allowed, but only at the viewpoint. Follow the guards' instructions, and do not fly a drone.
- Reported closed on **Wednesday**.
- Go early in the day for the clearest view.
- Best time, as first written: Morning (about 9–11 am), when it is clearest, before the afternoon haze

## Nearby / combine with
