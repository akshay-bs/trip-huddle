# Srimanta Sankardev Kalakshetra

## Overview
A large cultural complex in Panjabari, east Guwahati, named after the 15th–16th-century Assamese saint, poet and reformer Srimanta Sankardev. Built in the 1990s, it has a museum of Assamese and north-eastern culture, an art gallery, a heritage park with model traditional houses, open-air theatres, a library and eateries. It is Northeast India's biggest cultural centre.

## Why go
- A quick, easy introduction to Assam and the Northeast: costumes, masks, instruments, crafts, and model tribal houses.
- **Teens:** the heritage park and big gateway make for good photos; occasional dance or music performances in the evenings.
- **Adults:** the museum and art gallery; Assamese snacks at the stalls.
- It's on the Guwahati–Kaziranga side of the city, so it fits on the airport → Kaziranga drive or the city tour.

## Season reality
Good any time in December. Mild and dry. Check for special cultural events or fairs held around winter.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** easy loop around the campus and galleries, about 1 km in total.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** paved paths; a few steps at gallery entrances.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at the canteen or in the vehicle.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** city road; on-site parking.

## Activities
- Central museum (Assamese history, Vaishnavite culture, crafts)
- Heritage park with model traditional houses of the Northeast
- Art gallery; open-air theatre shows when scheduled
- Assamese snacks at the food stalls

## Tips
- With a 5–6 hr drive to Kaziranga still ahead, only stop if you land by late morning.
- Fees vary a little between sources (₹20–30 adult). Carry cash.
- Best time, as first written: Morning or early afternoon

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Assam State Museum]]: the other big museum, in the city centre
- [[Navagraha Temple]] and [[Umananda Temple]]: rest of the city tour
- Arista by Ambition: the proposed Guwahati hotel, about 5–6 km away
