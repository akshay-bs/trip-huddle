# Umiam Lake

## Overview
Umiam Lake, locally called Barapani, is a large reservoir created by damming the Umiam River in the early 1960s. It sits in Ri-Bhoi district right beside the Guwahati–Shillong highway (NH-6), about 15 km north of Shillong, and is the traditional "welcome to Meghalaya" stop. There are two ways to visit it:
- **Roadside viewpoints on NH-6:** a free photo stop above the lake with tea and snack stalls, about 10–20 min.
- **Umiam Water Sports Complex** next to the government-run **Orchid Lake Resort** (Meghalaya Tourism), with Lum Nehru Park nearby. This is the place for boating, a lakeside restaurant and a hillside pool open to visitors.

## Why go
- Big, calm views of blue water ringed by pine-covered Khasi hills. It is the first "Meghalaya" photo of the trip.
- Teens: speed boat, water scooter and kayaks at the water sports complex.
- Adults: tea with a view at the viewpoint or the Orchid Lake Resort restaurant. It is a relaxing break on the Guwahati–Shillong drive.

## Season reality
- The lake is at its dry-season level, and the clear skies give strong blue colours on sunny days.
- Mornings can be foggy or misty over the water, which clears by late morning.
- It is cold, especially on the water or in a speed boat (about 8–18 °C). Bring windproof layers.
- Weekends and Christmas week are busier, and the highway at Barapani can be congested.
- Verdict: a good, easy stop in either direction. It isn't a destination in itself.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** the viewpoint is essentially at the roadside. At the water sports complex it is a short walk down to the jetty and back (about 300 m round trip).
- **Surface / steps / railings:** paved paths, with some steps down to the boarding jetty.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** the jetty and boat boarding can be wet. Life jackets are provided.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at the resort restaurant, the park or the tea stalls.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** directly off NH-6. The tempo traveller can use roadside pull-offs or the complex parking. Highway pull-offs are informal, so the driver should choose a safe spot.

## Activities
- Photo stop at the NH-6 viewpoints (free)
- Water sports at the Umiam Water Sports Complex, about 9 AM – 5 PM: pedal boats, kayaks, motorboat or speed boat, water scooter, sometimes a larger cruise boat
- Lunch or tea at the Orchid Lake Resort restaurant, and a look at the hillside pool
- Lum Nehru Park stroll (lakeside garden)
- Boathouse stay on Lumpongdeng island (advance booking, max 6). Not relevant for a day stop

## Tips
- Choose the water sports complex only if the teens want a ride and there is time before dark.
- The [[Brahmaputra Sunset Cruise]] leaves around 4 PM in winter, so Guwahati traffic makes timing tight.
- Water sports prices change often, and older blog lists (₹20–200) are out of date. Ask for the full rate card at the counter and confirm per-person vs per-boat pricing.
- Carry cash. Some stalls don't take UPI when the network is weak.
- Best time, as first written: Late morning to afternoon for clear light. Sunset is also popular
