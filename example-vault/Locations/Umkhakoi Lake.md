# Umkhakoi Lake

## Overview
A small man-made reservoir formed by a dam on the Umkhakoi river, next to Mawlyngbna village, about 15 km beyond Mawsynram and roughly 75–80 km (2–2.5 hrs) from Shillong. It is surrounded by forested hills and rocky banks. Locals and tour operators call it the "Umkhakoi Water Park". It is the base for Mawlyngbna's water activities, not an amusement park with slides.

## Why go
- **Easy water fun:** beginner kayaking (about 30-min sessions) and rowing boats, with life jackets and guides. Adults can do the gentle rowing; teens will want the kayaks.
- **Unusual rocks:** the banks have natural potholes full of water, linked by bamboo bridges, and fossils (sea urchins, shells) in the exposed rock around the edges. Mawlyngbna sits on an ancient seabed.
- **Picnic setting:** a viewing deck and a quiet spot with few outside tourists.
- There is no clear evidence of a reed lake; sources describe open water with rocky, forested banks.

## Season reality
Dry season is the recommended season (Oct–Apr). The water is calm and the rocks are dry, so kayaking and boating are at their safest. The water will be cold. Temperatures are not published, but expect it to feel chilly in late December: fine for kayaking, very cold for swimming or pack-rafting. It is well off the Christmas-week crowd circuit. Operators do not run activities in bad weather, and the community may be quieter on Sundays and around 24–25 Dec, so pre-book through Mawlyngbna Traveller's Nest.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** a short walk from the road or parking to the lakeside platforms; allow ~20 min in total. Exact distance not published.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** rocky shores, some rough steps, and bamboo bridges over the potholes (few railings).
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** wet rock at the edge of the water and the potholes. Watch footing on the bamboo bridges.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes. Non-paddlers can sit on the viewing deck or rocks.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** Shillong → Mawsynram → Mawlyngbna road. The last stretch is narrow but motorable; a tempo traveller should manage (confirm with the driver).

## Activities
- Kayaking (beginner-friendly, about 30 min), rowing boats
- Swimming, pack-rafting and cliff jumping or rappelling at the lake, run by local guides with life jackets (water will be cold in December)
- Angling (local; ask in advance)
- Look for fossils in the rock at the lake edges. Look and photograph only; do not take any.

## Tips
- Book activities and guides in advance through Mawlyngbna Traveller's Nest. A local guide is required for every activity.
- Carry quick-dry clothes, a towel and a warm layer for after the water. There are no ATMs in the village, so carry cash (nearest ATM is in Mawsynram).
- Life jackets for everyone on the water, including strong swimmers.
- Grippy sandals or old sneakers for the rocks.

## Nearby / combine with
- It is part of the [[Mawlyngbna]] visit, on the same half-day as the village activities (canyoning, split rock, fossil trail). Do the lake in the afternoon and the walks in the morning.
- Mawjymbuin Cave at Mawsynram is on the way from Shillong/Sohra.
