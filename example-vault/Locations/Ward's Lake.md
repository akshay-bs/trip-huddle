# Ward's Lake

## Overview
A small, horseshoe-shaped artificial lake in central Shillong, next to Raj Bhavan (the Governor's residence), with a garden around it. The locals call it Nan Polok. It was built in 1894 under Chief Commissioner Sir William Ward. It has cobbled walking paths, a wooden bridge across the middle, a fountain, paddle boats and a botanical garden.

## Why go
- A green, peaceful break right in town, just a few minutes' walk from Police Bazaar.
- **Teens:** paddle boating, feeding the fish from the bridge, photos.
- **Adults:** an easy, flat stroll with benches and a café. It is a good wind-down after a long drive.

## Season reality
The weather is fine: sunny afternoons and cold in the shade. Fewer flowers are in bloom than in spring, and the lake is just pleasant rather than special. The main catch is the **early winter closing time (around 5–5:30 pm)**. It can be busy with locals and families on Christmas-week afternoons.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** a flat loop of about 1 km around the lake and across the bridge (20–40 min).
- **Surface / steps / railings:** cobbled and paved paths, with a few gentle slopes. The bridge has railings.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** none significant.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at the café or on the benches, or at Police Bazaar nearby.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** drop-off at the gate in central Shillong. Parking is tight, and it is easier to walk over from Police Bazaar.

## Activities
- Paddle boating, about 20–30 min per boat. Boating can stop earlier than the park closes.
- Walk the cobbled loop and cross the wooden bridge
- Feed the fish (puffed rice is sold at the entrance)
- Botanical garden and orchids, and the café

## Tips
- **Closed on Tuesday.** Winter hours are roughly 8:30 am–5:30 pm. Arrive by 4 pm to have time for boating.
- **Entry fee:** sources differ between ₹10 and ₹20 for adults, with a reduced rate for children and seniors. Paddle-boat rates were not verified, so carry cash.
- Pair it with the Police Bazaar walk: do the lake first, then shop and eat as the lights come on.
- Best time, as first written: Afternoon (around 2–4 pm), well before closing

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Police Bazaar]]: a short walk away, for shopping and dinner
