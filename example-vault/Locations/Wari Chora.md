# Wari Chora

> [!warning] A different corner of Meghalaya: it costs about 3 of the 8 days
> Wari Chora is in **South Garo Hills**, in the far west of the state, about 135 km west of Shillong as the crow flies. Every other place on the list is in the Khasi or Jaintia Hills, or in Assam. Reaching it means a **7–9 hr drive** from Guwahati or Shillong, **two nights** in basic village homestays (Emangre) or at Baghmara, and a 4x4 transfer for the last 15 km. The walk ends in a near-vertical bamboo-ladder descent, with a hard climb back out. It would replace most of a Shillong/Sohra/Dawki segment.

## Overview
A narrow gorge cut by the Rongdik river (a tributary of the Simsang) near Emangre village in South Garo Hills. Sheer rock walls rise either side of still, emerald-green water, and you explore it by inflatable kayak or raft with local guides. The nearest town is Baghmara (36 km). It is not well marked on Google Maps.

## Why go
- **The canyon:** paddle between tall, mossy rock walls on clear green water, with a small waterfall in a side crevasse. Around midday, sunlight on the waterfall can make rainbows.
- **Teens:** a real adventure: 4x4 ride, jungle walk, bamboo ladders, kayaking, and optional swimming with the guides.
- **Adults:** one of the most striking and least crowded places in Meghalaya, but only for those happy with a long, rough two-day detour.

## December reality
December is the best time to go. The canyon is closed April–October because the monsoon swells the river; in the dry months the water is low, calm and at its greenest. The dry track also helps the 4x4s. The water is cold, so anyone who swims or wades will be chilly, and the canyon floor gets sun only around midday. Boating stops at 2 pm and sunset is around 4:30 pm, so the day has to start early from Emangre. Christmas-week crowds are not an issue here; few people make the trip.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** about 1.5 km and roughly 1 hr each way from the parking area. One blogger gives 3–4 km in total; one tour operator calls it "easy", and others say "moderate to difficult". I've put 3 km and 120 min round trip in the properties.
- **Surface / steps / railings:** forest path, mostly flat at first, then a near-80° drop into the gorge on locally built bamboo ladders and stairs. There are no proper railings. Some wading in the river.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** the bamboo ladders and wet rocks at the river's edge. Guides carry the rafts over rocks between pools, and the narrowest stretch is kayak-only (two-seaters).
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, at Emangre or at the trailhead snack huts. But the whole group still has to make the long drive to the Garo Hills.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** the tempo traveller could probably reach Emangre via Baghmara (confirm with the driver; the roads include long rough sections). From Emangre, about 15 km of very bad off-road track (45–55 min) needs locally hired 4x4s. One cyclist recorded about 820 m of climbing over 26 km on the ride in from Emangre.

## Activities
- Kayak or raft through the canyon with local guides (about 2–4 hrs on the water)
- See the small waterfall in a side crevasse by kayak (rainbows around midday)
- Swim in the calm pools where the guides allow (very cold in December)
- The 4x4 ride and jungle walk to the river

## Tips
- Book through a homestay or operator in Emangre or Baghmara; a guide is needed and the place is hard to find on maps.
- Wear quick-dry clothes and trekking shoes with good grip; you will wade. Take your shoes off in the boat.
- Life jackets are provided; wear them.
- Carry a waterproof phone pouch, water and snacks, plus a dry change of clothes and a warm layer.
- Homestays in Emangre are very basic. Baghmara (36 km away) has slightly better options, and there's a homestay at Siju.
- Be off the river by 2 pm and back on the road before dark.

## Nearby / combine with
- Siju Caves (Siju, on the Simsang) and Balpakram National Park are in the same district; Muddie Trails' package combines Wari Chora with Siju Caves. Neither is in the vault.
- None of the vault's other places are close. The drive in from Shillong goes west via Nongstoin, away from [[Laitlum Canyons]], Sohra and Dawki.
