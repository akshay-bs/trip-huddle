# Wei Sawdong Falls

## Overview
A three-tiered "step" waterfall in the forest west of Sohra (Cherrapunji), off the road toward Dainthlen. The water drops down three rock ledges into turquoise pools. You can only see it properly from the bottom, which means a steep walk down into the gorge and back up.

## Why go
- One of the most photogenic falls in Meghalaya. The three tiers and blue-green pools are the Instagram shot.
- Teens will love the scramble down and the bottom pool.
- Adults: beautiful, but only worth it for those comfortable with a steep climb back up.

## December reality
Dry season. Flow is far thinner than in Oct–Nov, and by late December each tier may be a thin stream or a few ribbons. The upside is that the pools are clearest and bluest and the trail is drier, so it is less slippery than in the monsoon. Christmas-week crowds are moderate, as it is less famous than Nohkalikai.

## Access & walk
- **Walk:** From the trailhead parking, a steep path down to the base of the falls. About 20–30 min down and 30–45 min back up. Plan 60–90 min round trip plus time at the bottom. Distance is short but steep (exact distance and step count not reliably published).
- **Surface / steps / railings:** Mix of rough stone steps, beaten-earth slopes, tree roots and bamboo ladders/railings. The bamboo railings are makeshift and not everywhere.
- **Slippery or exposed sections:** Yes. Damp soil and moss make the steps slippery even in winter. The bamboo ladder sections need both hands. Rocks around the pool are slippery. Do not climb onto the upper tiers.
- **Can part of the group skip it and wait?** Yes, and this is recommended. The falls cannot be seen from the top, so those who wait only see the forest. They could wait at the stalls, or the group can split, with teens and fitter adults going down while others do [[Arwah Cave]] or [[Nohkalikai Falls]] nearby.
- **Last-mile road / vehicle access:** Paved road on the Sohra–Dainthlen side; a tempo traveller should reach the trailhead parking (confirm with the driver).

## Activities
- Walk down to the base and photograph all three tiers
- Sit by the bottom pool. Swimming is not advised: the water is cold in December, and the pool is deep with slippery rock edges.

## Tips
- Proper trekking shoes or sports shoes with grip. No sandals or smooth soles.
- Keep your hands free (use a backpack) for the bamboo ladders.
- Carry water. There is nothing at the bottom.
- Go before 1 pm. The gorge gets dark early in December, and you do not want to climb out in fading light.
- Anyone with knee problems should skip it; the steep descent is harder on knees than the climb.

## Nearby / combine with
- [[Nohkalikai Falls]] and [[Arwah Cave]] are on the same (west) side of Sohra.
- [[Mawsmai Cave]] and [[Seven Sisters Falls]] are south of Sohra town.
