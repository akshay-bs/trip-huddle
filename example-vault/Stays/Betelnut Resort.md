# Betelnut Resort

## Summary
A riverside resort in a betel-nut plantation at [[Shnongpdeng]], the village upstream of Dawki on the crystal-clear Umngot river. It is about a 5-minute walk down to the river bed and the boating point. Every room has a balcony. There are Deluxe (king), Standard Twin and Budget rooms, deluxe cottages and riverside camping tents, plus the *Betelnut Restaurant*, 24×7 room service and Wi-Fi. The resort arranges boating, kayaking and cliff-jumping. **Ratings:** Google 4.2 (692 reviews), TripAdvisor 4/5 (#1 in Shnongpdeng). The group's list **ticks** this one and notes **₹4,500**.
**Closeness to sights:** The best location on the list for the river. [[Umngot River (Dawki)]] boating and Shnongpdeng water sports are right below. Dawki town and the India–Bangladesh border are about 8–10 km downstream. [[Mawlynnong Village]] and [[Riwai Living Root Bridge]] are about 1–1.5 hrs away.

## Pros
- Right on the Umngot: early-morning boating before the crowds, with no long drive.
- Polite, helpful staff, and good food in a clean restaurant with a view.
- Rooms are big and nice, each with a balcony.
- Activities are organised on site.

## Cons / red flags from reviews
- **Steep steps:** the Standard rooms are "far down" a number of steep steps. There is no lift, and it is "not ideal for elderly".
- **Non-AC property with fans/coolers.** Heaters are not mentioned. The valley is warmer than Shillong (about 12–15 °C at night) but still cool. Ask about heaters and geysers.
- Lower rooms have limited views.
- Some guests mention ongoing construction work.
- A deluxe cottage was quoted at ₹8,000 plus ₹2,100 food for 2 days. Confirm what a ₹4,500 rate covers.
- Dawki gets very crowded in late December.
