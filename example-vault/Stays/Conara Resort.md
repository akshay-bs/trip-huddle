# Conara Resort

## Summary
A small, recently built guest-house style "resort", also listed as "Conara Resort Lake View" (Booking/Agoda) and possibly as "Conara Guest House" (Justdial). OTAs call it a 1-star property or a bed & breakfast. **It is not in Mawlynnong village.** It is on the Pynursla–Dawki highway at Nongshyrngan/Pynter, about 7–8 km in a straight line north-east of Mawlynnong. By road that is a short drive to the Mawlynnong turn-off and on towards Dawki. **Capacity is the big issue.** Booking.com says it has about 6 rooms, with doubles and one triple type, and no extra beds. Ratings: Google 4.1 (94 reviews), but Goibibo 3.3/5 (4 ratings), MakeMyTrip about 2.0/5 (2 ratings), and Booking/Agoda 1.0 (1 review, see red flags).
**Closeness to sights:** It is on the main road between the sights, not beside them. [[Mawlynnong Village]] and [[Riwai Living Root Bridge]] are a short drive away, down the Mawlynnong side road. [[Umngot River (Dawki)]] is further down the same highway. MakeMyTrip gives about 1 hr to Wah Umngot, while Goibibo claims 5.3 km, which does not match the map.

## Pros
- On the Pynursla–Dawki road, so it works as a transit night between Mawlynnong/Dawki and the next day's Jowai run.
- New-looking building with balconies and mountain/lake views. Photos show modern interiors.
- Google reviewers rate it fairly well overall (4.1 from 94).
- Restaurant and room service are listed (Goibibo).
- The Goibibo rate had free cancellation when checked.

## Cons / red flags from reviews
- **Scam warning:** An Agoda/Booking review (Jan 2026) says the hotel does **not** accept bookings through Booking.com and that scammers listed it there to take advance money. Book only via a verified phone number, and never pay an advance to an unverified OTA/WhatsApp contact.
- **Too small for a big group:** About 6 rooms. The Standard room is tiny (~100 sq ft, one double bed) and there are no extra beds.
- **Heating / hot water unknown.** The listings mention "air conditioning" (maybe a heat-pump AC?), but no heaters or geysers are confirmed. This roadside spot at about 1,000 m+ will be cold at night, so confirm.
- **Parking conflict:** Goibibo lists parking, but Booking.com says "no parking available". A 17-seater may have to stand on the highway shoulder.
- **Wi-Fi conflict:** Goibibo lists Wi-Fi, but Agoda says it is unavailable.
- Very few detailed written reviews exist. Goibibo/MakeMyTrip scores are low on a tiny sample. The Agoda/Booking scores (2.5/10 in every category) come from the single scam-warning review.
- Veg food depends on a small kitchen. There is no menu information.
