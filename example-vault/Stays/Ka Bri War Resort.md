# Ka Bri War Resort

## Summary
A well-rated resort about 100 m off the Pynursla–Dawki highway (NH206) at Nongshyrngan, between Pynursla and Dawki. It is practically next door to [[Conara Resort]]: Conara's Booking.com pin is on the same spot. It has large, modern, very clean rooms, all with mountain-view balconies, and two restaurants. One is **pure vegetarian**, and reviewers call it the only pure-veg kitchen in the Dawki area. A pool and more cottages were under construction. **Ratings:** Google 4.5 (342 reviews), plus TripAdvisor and Agoda listings. The group's list wrote it as "Kabriwar" (1 night, right-hand column).
**Closeness to sights:** A "highway" base, not a riverside one. [[Mawlynnong Village]] and [[Riwai Living Root Bridge]] are about 30–40 min. [[Umngot River (Dawki)]] and [[Shnongpdeng]] are about 45–60 min down the ghat road. It suits a Mawlynnong + Dawki day with an overnight in between.

## Pros
- Top reviews for cleanliness, linen ("comparable to any 5-star property"), polite and well-managed staff, and a lovely buffet breakfast.
- A **pure-veg restaurant**, a big plus for vegetarians.
- Large, well-appointed rooms with stunning balcony views.
- A strong alternative to [[Conara Resort]], which is at the same spot but is small and has scam warnings.
- Right on the highway, so easy for the tempo traveller.

## Cons / red flags from reviews
- One TripAdvisor review is titled "Highly overpriced and average food". Several call the restaurant a bit expensive.
- Ongoing construction (pool, new rooms) may mean noise.
- Heating and hot water are not described. At about 1,000 m the nights will be cold, so ask.
- Room count is not published.
- Aggregator rates are about US$53–74 (≈ ₹4,500–6,200). ₹5,000 is a middle guess.
