# Polo Cherrapunjee Resort

## Summary
Formerly **Polo Orchid Resort**, now "Polo Cherrapunjee Resort" (Polo Hotels & Resorts). It is the best-known upmarket resort in Cherrapunjee, on the cliff edge about 50 m from Nohsngithiang ([[Seven Sisters Falls]]). It has stone-and-timber buildings with big windows, rooms, suites with jacuzzis, villas and log huts with private plunge pools, an **infinity pool**, a spa, a restaurant, live music and bonfires. **Ratings:** TripAdvisor 4.6/5 from 2,197 reviews (very high volume). The group's list wrote it as "Polla Resort".
**Closeness to sights:** Right beside [[Seven Sisters Falls]]. [[Mawsmai Cave]] and [[Mawsmai Nongthymmai Eco Park]] are a few minutes away. [[Nohkalikai Falls]] is about 8–10 km. The Sohra market is about 8 km.

## Pros
- A large, established resort with thousands of reviews. The whole group fits easily.
- Stunning cliff-edge views of the falls, the valley and the Bangladesh plains.
- Infinity pool, spa, bonfires and live music: plenty for the teens in the evenings.
- Most reviewers call the rooms spacious, well appointed and tastefully done.

## Cons / red flags from reviews
- **Expensive:** from about US$118 (≈ ₹9,800), with an average about US$245 in peak season. Several reviewers feel the price is not matched by the value.
- Some rooms feel **outdated**, with old-fashioned decor (Jan 2025 review), and occasional maintenance issues.
- Heaters are not confirmed. The rooms are described as air-conditioned, which may mean heat-pump AC. Ask for room heaters.
- The infinity pool is outdoor, so it is unlikely to be usable in December unless heated.
- A **second TripAdvisor listing, "Polo Resort Cherrapunji"**, exists.
