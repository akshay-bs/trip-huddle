# Ri Kynjai Serenity by the Lake

## Summary
A 4-star resort on 45 acres of forest above [[Umiam Lake]], about 20 km north of Shillong on the Guwahati side.

## Pros
- Best-known "nature" stay near Shillong: lake views, forest, Khasi-style cottages with upturned-boat roofs.
- Each cottage has a fireplace, which matters on a 5–8 °C December night.
- Restaurant (Sao Aiom: Indian, Chinese, Continental), bar and spa on site.

## Cons / red flags from reviews
- **Price:** published rates are ₹9,500 (Supreme), ₹11,000 (Superior), ₹14,500 (Lake View Cottage) per night, and an OTA showed about ₹13,800 + ₹4,080 tax for a forest-view room. These may be older rates. 7 rooms at the lowest rate is about ₹66,500 for one night, much more than the other options.
- Only 17 rooms, so book early for Christmas week.
- Rooms are fan-cooled (no AC, not needed in December). Veg menu not checked.
- Reviews not yet checked for this note.
