# Sohra Plaza

## Summary
A small family-run homestay (sometimes listed as "Sohra Plaza Resort"; Google calls it a 3-star hotel) on the Sohra–Shella road, south of Sohra town near Sa-I-Mika Park. It sits beside a stream with open views of the hills. Ratings: Google 4.2, TripAdvisor 4.1/5 (31 reviews, #4 of 16 speciality lodgings in Sohra), MakeMyTrip about 3.3/5.
**Closeness to sights:** It is on the southern (Mawsmai) side of Sohra. [[Mawsmai Cave]] is about 2 km away (Agoda), and [[Seven Sisters Falls]] is on the same stretch nearby. [[Nohkalikai Falls]] is a short drive. [[Kongthong Whistling Village]] is a long day-trip, a couple of hours each way, so check the drive time.

## Pros
- Scenic, quiet setting beside a stream. Reviewers repeatedly praise the views, especially from the 1st-floor rooms.
- The listing (Agoda/Yatra) says every room has a **room heater**, a balcony and hot and cold running water. A backup generator is listed.
- Staff (the host "Mr Sunny" is often named) are described as warm and accommodating. Food is cooked fresh to order and a campfire is offered at no extra cost.
- "Lot of space for parking" (TripAdvisor). This suits a 17-seater, but confirm, since the road is a narrow hill highway.
- Very cheap listed tariffs (₹1,000–2,000 per room).

## Cons / red flags from reviews
- **Capacity:** Only 5 rooms, all Non-AC.
- **Power:** One guest reports "no electricity" for two days and relying on an inverter, with no light in the toilets and trouble charging phones. The generator claim needs confirming. In December, a power cut also means heaters stop working.
- **Hygiene:** Some reviews report pests and unclean bedding, while others call it "very clean". Results look inconsistent.
- **Wi-Fi:** Reviews from 2017 and 2021 say there is no Wi-Fi, or it is extremely weak, despite the listing claiming free Wi-Fi.
- **Food service is slow.** One reviewer says meal orders "take hours". Veg options depend on asking. It is a Khasi homestay kitchen with no printed menu.
- **Remote:** Few eating places nearby, so everything needs the vehicle.
- Several listings and names exist (Sohra Plaza / Sohra Plaza Resort / Sohra Plaza Homestay; two TripAdvisor pages).
- Tariffs on hotelsincherrapunjee.com may be out of date. Expect higher December rates.
