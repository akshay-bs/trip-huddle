# Trip itinerary

> What `build-itinerary` writes once the group has chosen a route and `plan-day` has planned every day. This excerpt shows two days of an invented Portugal trip: the reference Day notes of `plan-day` (Day 04) and `review-travel-times` (Day 05). A real one has every day of the trip, in order.

## Day 4 · Wed 9 Jun · Sunrise cliff, harbour boat trip, old town
- **Start:** 5:00 am, leave the guesthouse in Lagos, before sunrise.
- **Stops, in order:** [[Cliff Viewpoint (Example)]], 5:45 to 7:15 am, sunrise from the level path · breakfast in the village · [[Fishing Harbour (Example)]], 9:30 am to 2:00 pm, boat trip and lunch · free time at the guesthouse from 3:00 pm · [[Old Town Walk (Example)]] at 5:00 pm, flat, about 2 hrs.
- **Meals:** breakfast at the village café after the viewpoint; lunch at the harbour, booked for the group; dinner in the old town.
- **Along the way:** the lighthouse on the coast road, seen from the van; the only fuel station on the route, at the harbour roundabout.
- **End:** Lagos, [[Example Guesthouse Lagos]]. Back by 3:00 pm; sunset 8:54 pm.
- **Drive:** about 75 km, up to 2¼ hrs in all.

## Day 5 · Thu 10 Jun · Mountain Fort and a cork oak farm
- **Start:** 8:30 am, leave the guesthouse in Lagos after breakfast.
- **Stops, in order:** [[Mountain Fort (Example)]], 10:45 am to 1:00 pm · [[Cork Oak Farm (Example)]], 1:45 to 4:30 pm, tour and lunch.
- **Meals:** breakfast at the guesthouse; lunch at the farm, booked for the group; dinner in Lagos old town.
- **Along the way:** the old aqueduct on the road out of Lagos; the last café before the hills, at the turn-off for the fort.
- **End:** Lagos, [[Example Guesthouse Lagos]]. Back by 5:50 pm; sunset 8:54 pm.
- **Drive:** about 95 km, about 4¾ hrs with stops. The hill road to the fort is slower than a map says.
