## Wishes covered
- **Must: at least two beach days.** Long Sand Beach and Lagoon Beach, in the Algarve. Golden Cove Beach is a beach too, but has steep steps.
- **Must: one old town with a castle.** Walled Hill Town, in the Alentejo. Castle Hill Lanes in Lisbon is one too, but steep. [[Mountain Fort (Example)]] is already in the vault.
- **Nice: a winery visit.** Estate Winery, near Évora.
- **Nice: a boat trip into sea caves.** Sea Caves Boat Trip, from Lagos. It runs only when the sea is calm, which is usual in June.
- **Avoid: long climbs or steep steps.** Palace in the Hills and Golden Cove Beach don't fit for this reason. Castle Hill Lanes is mixed.
- **Avoid: more than 3 hours of driving in a day.** Every place below is under 2 hours from its base. Lake Beach is the furthest, about 1.5 hours each way from Évora.
